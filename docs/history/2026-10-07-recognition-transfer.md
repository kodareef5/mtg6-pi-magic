# Recognition through the production planner

The failed generation arms do not isolate the full answer schema as the cause.
Plain-language tactics, compact questions and small action proposals also made
losing choices. Candidate-count experiments asked Luna to invent alternatives;
consequence feedback asked it to repair a line without supplying a replacement.
Neither tested whether the earlier successful recognition survives the normal
planning contract. A new playbook contract and serial construction also remain
untested, but require more machinery than this diagnostic.

This experiment supplies the original four checked blocked-position lines.
Two win within their stated no-response scope; two do not. It tests recognition
and preservation of supplied choices, not search, generated tactics or general
playing strength. Both committee reviewers endorsed this bounded next question.

alone does not prove target-policy obedience. This establishes expressibility, not whether the
writer can discover that expression.

Relative to production, transfer may change only the added alternatives and

## Gate fixed before inference

Run three minimal controls at the clean experimental commit. Continue to the
three transfers only if every control selects a checked winning alternative.
No selective regeneration or fallback selection is allowed. For each transfer,
record selection and preservation separately:

- A winning alternative is selected and fully encoded.
- A winning alternative is named, but commitments are missing or changed.
- A nonwinning alternative is selected.
- Every alternative is declined and another line is written.
- Selection is ambiguous, or no accepted submission exists.

Require all three transfers to preserve a winning commitment with consistent
delivered payment, waiting, target, trigger and completion policies. Record
false audit rationale separately from execution-policy defects. An omitted
future Smaug attack cannot identify discoverability versus construction as its
cause, even though the offline witness proves that the syntax permits it.
Payments in the supplied alternatives are examples. Equivalent feasible payments
count. Candidate 0 needs its Sanctuary attack commitment and no authored payment
or other policy that taps that attacker before declaration. A separate Sanctuary
hold is useful but not required; record whether it exists. Candidate 1 needs both
casts paid from distinct resources. Every explicit payment policy must be coherent,
and physical controls grade whether the pilot follows it.

The runner retains the old case checks as diagnostic flags only for transfer.
They require Sanctuary and would reject candidate 1's valid Challenger attack.
Transfer runner PASS means an accepted plan, not preservation or a winning line;
the manual gate above controls expansion. Any failure or ambiguity stops this
stage, without automatic upkeep expansion.

Only three passing transfers proceed to bounded physical controls through the
shared runner. Check actual commitments and payments, Red repair dependence,
all-seat help, legality, gaps, fallback, replay and clone. Report those measures
separately from the observed winner. Record calls, syntax/resource refusals,
latency, tokens, cost, and unmetered requests throughout.

A positive result nominates a later feasibility test for a generic candidate
generator using accepted mechanics. It does not adopt a solver, a mandatory
review, a pregame template library or a full-game gate. A negative result stops
this transfer approach without assigning a cause the experiment did not isolate.
