## Experimental contract

The shared runner's `block-pairs` arm uses the ordinary preparation writer and
validators. It adds exact reusable `block:<blocker-id>:<attacker-id>` actions
for each own current creature and each opposing current creature. It does not
rank pairs or filter keyword conflicts, tapped creatures or sickness. It keeps
the broad selector and labels it as matching any attacker.

Pairs follow physical cards, not incarnations. A source query keeps the blocker
on its controller's battlefield; the actual offered option determines whether
the pair is available. No new plan schema or physical option is introduced.
Matching never certifies the complete blocking declaration. A future creature
not currently on the battlefield has no pair entry. Production defaults stay
unchanged. Preparation retains the existing forecast assumption that current
permanents survive; it does not simulate pending combat damage before making
the pair catalog.

The exact option matters: a constructed prefix can collide with a longer id.
Two refs in an ordinary object query mean any overlap, not both participants.
The catalog makes precise selection discoverable; the prior vocabulary was
not completely unable to express it. In particular, `block:done` was already
available, so pair entries do not explain its omission in the earlier failure.

## Bounded gate

Generate three preparations through the shared runner at a clean experimental
commit. Keep the prescribed roster, prompt, reply budget, policies and facts.
Compare each first live request with the frozen treatment request, ignoring
message timestamps only. There is no control arm: choosing a new pair key is
attributable to its availability; condition correctness and strategic quality
are not measured improvements over control.

Before physical continuations, inspect each accepted plan:

- Existing-card attacker-specific intent must use the exact pair, with coherent
  conditions and explicit declaration completion. A broad binding that still
  means one attacker, false blocking restrictions, or missing completion fails.
- A plan without attacker-specific blocking intent is inconclusive for pair
  binding. It is not a pass; its strategy is graded separately. Intent depending
  on a future creature outside this catalog is also outside this binding gate.
- Any failed or inconclusive construction stops live expansion. Do not regenerate
  selectively. Record syntax/refusal failures separately from policy errors.

Only three applicable construction passes proceed to the two supplied declaration
controls with those plans. Require the chosen pair, coherent completion, no
illegal block, no Green foreground repair, and replay/clone health. A policy
requiring a different board cannot be graded on the unchanged-Forest controls.
Record that mismatch as inconclusive, rather than silently changing its line.
The attacks and intervening passes are supplied inputs, not model strategy.

No result here adopts the arm by default, closes lethal generation or objection
coverage, demonstrates post-rollback recovery, or authorizes a full-game gate.
