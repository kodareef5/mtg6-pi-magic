# Objections to recorded blocks

The declaration recovery probes finalized the illegal lone Forest block on
menace Zhao three times. Red could request strategic help but had no direct
judge choice. Its next packets contained no recorded row or conflict hint.
The judge's request also omitted both participants' card texts: this block's
receipt has empty before/after snapshots.

## Contract

Core projects the latest finished block while it remains the latest decision,
in declare-blockers, at its receipt clock or the following ordinary priority
grant. The record identifies the row, exact participants and complete assignment.
Current traits and keyword conflicts are labelled current. They are not proof
of declaration-time legality: completing a block can change continuous effects.
A legal menace pair receives no single-blocker conflict hint.

When a judge is seated, Jev may choose `object:block:<row>` for another seat's
nonempty declaration. All physical options, inspection, help and rule routes
remain available. The objection consumes a reserved classifier slot, takes no
physical action and starts no planner. Legal blocks also permit an objection.
The judge may be wrong; offering a case does not certify it.

On a chosen objection to a combat declaration, core replays the prefix before
the contested row and projects it publicly. The judge receives the recorded
assignment, exact identities, accepted characteristics, public effects and full
card texts. Accepted terms remain interpretations for the judge to check.
Historical receipt shapes remain unchanged. Later traits or identities do not
replace declaration-time evidence. Reconstruction costs a replay, no inference.

A stand ruling suppresses this offered case on the current branch. A failed
judge request records `ruling: null` and a failure explanation, with no verdict
or rollback. That failure also suppresses the direct offer after resume;
explicit human or strategy objections retain their existing interface. A later
rollback to or before the contested row reopens the offer for a new declaration,
even when it reuses the row number. Reports distinguish unruled cases from
verdicts. Core never refers a case or chooses an objection.

This direct pilot reader covers blocks only. A later physical decision or a
control transition beyond the first priority grant closes it. It is not a
historical legality scanner.

## Preferred sequences

For the lone Forest block, Red reads the assignment and current menace hint,
chooses an objection, and the judge checks the pre-declaration evidence. An
upheld objection returns Green to its pending declaration, with withdrawal
available. Both seats receive the existing replan request. Green must explicitly
withdraw or otherwise complete a legal declaration. The upheld ruling remains beside the unfinished declaration and its finish
option through withdrawals and reselections. It describes the contested action,
not the legality of the revised selection. A rollback alone is not recovery.

For Explorer and Hydra blocking Zhao together, the complete assignment has no
menace conflict. Jev can continue. If a seat nevertheless objects, the judge
must let this legal assignment stand. A block that changes an accepted ability
uses the reconstructed earlier characteristics, irrespective of current hints.

## Gate

Offline invariants cover purity, public evidence, exact historical identities,
blocking-dependent traits, the legal menace pair, option capacity and mapping,
no judge availability, physical and control boundaries, work edits, stand and
failure suppression, rollback row reuse, and failed-case save/replay/clone.
Required types, tests and saved prefixes must pass before live calls.

Run three pilot calls and three judge-only calls on each of the illegal lone
block and the legal pair. Then run three bounded continuations of the illegal
position. Record objection choice, judge correctness, rollback, withdrawal,
completed legal recovery and repeated declarations separately. Runner PASS means
replay/clone health only. Bound continuations by judge attempts as well as
recorded decisions: rollbacks can erase net ledger growth. Boundary stops leave
the physical decision pending and are neither losses nor fallback.

The legal pair is a supplied control, produced by recorded choices from the
carried version-zero opening: develop Green creatures, cast Zhao, attack only
with Zhao, then block with Explorer and Hydra. It is not evidence of model
strategy. Frozen inputs are in the shared benchmark manifest.
