# Checked consequence feedback

The upkeep lifecycle gate delivered both Lightning Strike copies, their budgets
and the Forest block arithmetic. Luna selected no upkeep win in three probes;
the supplied two-Strike line executed in all three controls. The remaining
question is whether a concrete consequence lets Luna repair its own line.

This diagnostic changes the benchmark only. It uses the production planner,
schema and validation, Jev, Luna low, carried pregame and summary off. There is
no challenger and no automatic line search. The two committee reviewers agreed
to test manually checked feedback before building an outcome calculator.

## Frozen inputs and receipts

Generate three ordinary initial plans in each of four cases at one clean
experimental commit: `red-upkeep-lethal`, `red-ready-lethal`,
`red-blocked-lethal-fresh` and `after-explorer-repair`. Keep every result, its
exact request, full proposal and resource receipt. Do not select convenient
failures or regenerate to obtain more mistakes.

Before any repair, classify each source as W (coherent winning commitments
verified within the receipt's scope), N (not W), or inconclusive. A reviewer
other than the receipt author checks the arithmetic and its exact candidate
hash. Green is a lasting-development guard, not a certified defensive rescue;
its usefulness and survival need a separate audit.

Both review arms receive the same original proposal as their amendment base,
current facts, resource receipt, instruction and production submission schema.
Only treatment adds a checked counterexample. Each arm has one reply, the
existing 4,000-token ceiling and timeout. Lookups close on that final reply.
An empty update confirms the proposal. A refused, truncated or missing answer
is unresolved: there is no fallback proposal and nothing to play.

The counterexample names one legal blocking assignment and conditional player
damage and life under unchanged characteristics and no responses. Authored burn
targets remain explicit assumptions, never purported locked targets. Future
entries and other unverified effects are listed as unchecked; a partial damage
sum is never presented as the whole line's outcome. If no counterexample exists
within those limits, say so without certifying a win. Do not propose another
line or append uncast hand cards beside the life calculation. The receipt is
informational, belongs to the original hash, and does not certify the repair.

## Gate

Upkeep and ready-lethal decide whether to expand. Each needs at least two N
sources; otherwise that case is inconclusive. Treatment must repair every N
source to coherent winning commitments, outperform control on N sources, and
preserve every W source. Accepted repairs must physically win without a later
Red writer supplying a missing commitment. Grade winning construction separately
from Jev's execution, including payment, targets and attack completeness.

Run through the opponent's next turn unless the game ends first. Record Red
help and all-seat help separately, draw amendments, gaps, fallback, replay and
clone, review latency and total cost. First-submission resource refusal is
separate from syntax/reference failure: a fresh review resets the production
budget warning and can exhaust the single reply on an unchanged proposal.

A failed or inconclusive deciding gate stops live expansion. Only a pass permits
the blocked-lethal risk check and Green development guard. Frozen unused inputs
remain recorded. No result at three repetitions adopts a production feature.
Treatment-only improvement nominates a scoped reader and another gate; both-arm
improvement nominates an extra-review gate. Neither improving ends this receipt
variant. Unchecked feedback or execution failure cannot establish that Luna
failed to use a correct consequence.

## Runner

Initial production results now include `commitment`. Reviewed receipts use
`{receipts: [{id, iteration, hash, initial, reviewedBy, counterexample}]}`;
`counterexample` contains `assumptions`, `consequence`, `unchecked` and
`inconclusive`. Classification and reviewer names stay outside model input.

```sh
npm run benchmark -- --live --repeat 3 --case red-upkeep-lethal \
  --arm receipt-paired --repair-source path/to/initial/results.json \
  --receipts path/to/reviewed-receipts.json --play --out path/to/paired
```

The runner preflights every selected hash and receipt before inference, then
alternates control and treatment order. The default production path is unchanged.
Offline invariants compare both full requests, preserve the original frame,
reject mismatched hashes and positions, and prove that an invalid submission
gets only one call and no replacement.
