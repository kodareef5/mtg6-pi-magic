# Jeskai Artifacts

Written 2026-10-11 by gpt-6.1-sol:high from the list at https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/7954760, cards generated 2026-10-10. Sideboardless alpha: main deck only. Advice for the pregame; the board and card text win any disagreement.

## 1. Archetype and game plan

Jeskai Artifacts is an engine-control deck: trade artifacts for time and cards, establish Simulacrum Synthesizer, then overwhelm combat with scaling Constructs. It normally develops on turns one through three and takes over from turn four onward; it is not an early aggro deck. The Fire Crystal supplies a haste finish, while Vehicles and Castle Doom provide alternative threats.

The 60 cards comprise 24 lands, 32 artifacts, and four United Battlefront. Of those artifacts, 31 are eligible Battlefront hits; only The Fire Crystal costs too much. Sixteen artifacts have mana value three, giving Synthesizer abundant triggers. Your central tension is deploying answers quickly enough to survive without spending the artifacts needed to build your engine.

## 2. Engine and key interactions

- **Synthesizer into Battlefront:** Cast Simulacrum Synthesizer for {2}{U}, then United Battlefront for {3}{W}. Each qualifying artifact entering produces a Construct. Battlefront putting Synthesizer and another three-mana artifact onto the battlefield simultaneously produces one Construct; two Synthesizers see each other and produce two. Constructs count themselves and one another: four other artifacts plus two Constructs means two 6/6s.
- **Repurposing Bay ladder:** Cast Bay for {2}{U}; each activation costs {2}, tapping Bay and sacrificing another artifact. A mana-value-zero token finds Candy Trail or Dusk Rose Reliquary; a one-mana artifact finds a two-mana artifact; Cryogen Relic finds a three-mana engine or answer. Relic also draws when sacrificed. Bay puts Reliquary directly onto the battlefield, avoiding its additional casting cost. Sacrificing a three-mana artifact finds The Fire Crystal; nothing here continues the ladder to five.
- **Cheap material:** Cryogen Relic costs {1}{U}, replaces itself on entry, and draws again whenever it leaves. Its {1}{U} sacrifice ability can delay a tapped attacker; choosing no target still permits the sacrifice. Candy Trail supplies selection, then costs {2} to cash in for life and a card. Both feed Bay, Castle Doom, crafting, and Reliquary.
- **Transformation:** Braided Net crafts for {1}{U}, exiling itself and another artifact permanent or graveyard card. Braided Quipu subsequently costs {3}{U} and a tap for a large refill. Its mana value remains three, so its transformed return triggers Synthesizer. Spring-Loaded Sawblades crafts for {3}{W}; Bladewheel Chariot retains mana value two and does not trigger Synthesizer.
- **Long-game blink:** Harness The Mind Stone for {5}{W} plus tapping it, using other mana sources. Its end-step blink redraws Cryogen Relic twice, resets Braided Net, or retriggers Synthesizer's scry. Blinking a three-mana artifact triggers other Synthesizers. Blinking removal can release its captive; blinking a transformed card returns its front face. Never blink a Construct expecting it to return.

## 3. The curve and sequencing by turn

**On the play:** Turn one, prefer Meticulous Archive when no immediate play matters; otherwise cast Candy Trail with an untapped land. Turn two, play The Mind Stone or Cryogen Relic. Turn three, establish Simulacrum Synthesizer if safe, or deploy Perilous Snare/Braided Net against pressure. A turn-two Stone permits four mana on turn three, including an accelerated Battlefront. Turn four, cast United Battlefront or pair a three-mana artifact with another cheap permanent. Turn five, develop Repurposing Bay and activate it for five total mana, or deploy additional three-mana artifacts before attacking with existing Constructs.

**On the draw:** Turn one still fixes and filters. Turn two may require holding {1}{W} for Spring-Loaded Sawblades rather than developing Stone. Turn three prioritize Snare, Net, or Pinnacle Starcage when behind. Turn four establish Synthesizer only if that does not concede combat; Battlefront can instead find two stabilizers. Turn five convert stabilization into Constructs or a Bay chain. These are branches, not a forced curve.

Hallowed Fountain and Meticulous Archive enable Floodfarm Verge's blue ability; Plains and Sacred Foundry do not. Lead Archive early rather than letting its tapped entry spoil a critical turn. Starting Town enters untapped only during your first three turns. Castle Doom fixes artifact spells, not Battlefront or activated abilities.

## 4. Mulligans

Keep two to four lands, functional colored mana, and both early development and a route to interaction or an engine. Three lands, a two-mana artifact, Synthesizer, and Battlefront or removal is a strong template. Two-land hands need cheap selection, a coherent curve, and realistic access to both blue and white.

Ship one-land hands, flooded hands without selection, and hands whose only action is expensive or conditional. Multiple Castle Doom/Petrified Hamlet can cast artifacts but cannot supply colored mana for Battlefront or Relic's activation. Against aggression, prioritize immediate defense over a beautiful but slow engine hand. Account for Pinnacle Starcage and Unidentified Hovership requiring two white mana.

## 5. Combat and defense

Attack when surviving artifact count leaves Constructs comfortably ahead of blockers; keep one back when racing would expose you to lethal. Every artifact removed shrinks every Construct immediately. Sacrificing a Construct also reduces the others' size, so calculate the post-sacrifice board before blocks or damage.

Braided Net can tap a blocker before combat or an attacker before attackers are declared. Its lock suppresses activated abilities while the permanent remains tapped, not triggered or static abilities. Sawblades answers a tapped creature, usually after attackers are declared and before damage.

Unidentified Hovership supplies flying pressure and crews for one. Bladewheel Chariot can animate by tapping two other untapped artifacts, conserving creatures for attacking or blocking. A newly entered Vehicle still cannot attack without haste. Castle Doom makes a stable 3/3 for {3}, tapping the land and sacrificing an artifact, but only at sorcery speed.

## 6. Resources and timing

Your instant-speed options are narrow: reserve {1}{W} for Sawblades, {1}{U} for Relic, or {2} for Candy Trail. Bay, crafting, Castle Doom, and Snare's counter ability are sorcery-speed commitments. Noncreature artifacts can use tap abilities immediately, including The Mind Stone after entry.

Snare starts speed at one; subsequent increases require opponent life loss on separate turns of yours. Multiple hits in one turn do not accelerate repeatedly. At maximum speed, add its counter before attacking.

The Fire Crystal can be found with Bay without paying red mana. Casting it needs {2}{R}{R}, which Castle Doom can help supply. Its copying activation needs actual {4}{R}{R}: only Sacred Foundry and Starting Town produce unrestricted red here, so normally both must be available.

## 7. What beats it and how to adapt

Fast go-wide pressure punishes setup. Find Pinnacle Starcage early, but recognize its symmetry: it exiles your Constructs, cheap artifacts, and Stone too. Exiled tokens will not return. Its eight-mana finishing activation creates Robots for cards put into graveyards, not vanished tokens.

Artifact destruction and bounce can simultaneously shrink Constructs and release creatures held by Snare or Reliquary. Avoid relying on one removal artifact to contain the entire opposing attack. Unidentified Hovership's departure instead gives the exiled creature's owner a manifest dread; it does not return that creature.

Countermagic punishes expensive Battlefront turns; removal aimed at Synthesizer punishes engine dependence. Against open interaction, develop independent artifacts and Bay rather than committing every resource to one payoff. Torpor Orb attacks opposing creature-entry engines but does not stop your normal noncreature artifact-entry triggers. Against control, preserve draw artifacts, threaten Castle Doom, and harness Stone when the mana investment is safe. No sideboard is supplied; these are main-deck adjustments.

## 8. Common mistakes

- Spending Castle Doom's colored mana on Battlefront or abilities.
- Expecting Relic, Stone, or Chariot to trigger Synthesizer.
- Sacrificing occupied removal without planning for the released permanent.
- Playing a second The Mind Stone as though both legendary copies can remain; use surplus copies as Bay material before deploying replacements.
- Sweeping away your own engine support with Starcage without calculating the resulting board.
- Naming Castle Doom with Petrified Hamlet and accidentally disabling your own token-making ability; its restriction affects every source with that name, though mana abilities remain usable.
- Forgetting that shrinking artifact count changes combat math immediately, or spending defensive mana on a sorcery-speed value line before surviving the next attack.
