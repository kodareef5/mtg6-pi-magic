# Azorius Momo

Written 2026-10-11 by gpt-6.1-sol:high from the list at https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/7991172, cards generated 2026-10-10. Sideboardless alpha: main deck only. Advice for the pregame; the board and card text win any disagreement.

## 1. Archetype and game plan

Azorius Momo is an explosive flying-creature tempo deck with a blink-value engine. Its 21 lands support cheap deployment rather than reliable five-mana casting: Momo, Friendly Flier discounts flyers, Springleaf Drum converts creatures into mana, and warp brings expensive creatures down early. Daydream then makes warped creatures permanent while repeating their enters triggers.

Win by assembling several flyers, attacking above ground defenses, and increasing damage with Cosmogrand Zenith, Guiding Hydra, or Abandoned Air Temple. Doubled Sage of the Skies stabilizes races. Quantum Riddler and Starfield Shepherd sustain development when the initial rush stalls. This is not a control deck: its five removal spells, 2 Get Lost + 2 Seam Rip + 1 Erode, clear crucial obstacles, not every opposing threat.

## 3. The curve and sequencing by turn

Lead Plains when available: it enables Floodfarm Verge's blue mana, Gleaming Bastion's continuing colored mana, and untapped Abandoned Air Temple. Hallowed Fountain enables Floodfarm Verge but is not basic. Gleaming Bastion produces colored mana on its entry turn even without a basic; beware later turns. Use tapped Hallowed Fountain when no immediate play warrants paying two life.

**On the play, a representative curve:**
1. Plains, Momo, Friendly Flier.
2. Second land, discounted warped Starfield Shepherd plus Daydream; alternatively use the three-mana Drum line above.
3. Cheap spell followed by discounted Sage of the Skies, or Cosmogrand Zenith to prepare later double-spelling.
4. Combine a value creature with removal or a second spell; attack before blinking any creature whose damage matters.
5. Continue pressure, recast a warped card from exile, or activate Abandoned Air Temple if a wide board makes that stronger than another body.

**On the draw:**
1. Establish Momo, Friendly Flier unless Seam Rip must stop an immediate engine.
2. Against pressure, prioritize Sage of the Skies setup or removal over pure card value; otherwise use the same warp-blink line.
3. Deploy lifelink bodies, ideally doubled, while keeping necessary removal available.
4. Stabilize the ground with expendable creatures and send flyers; use Haliya, Guided by Light when three life is achievable.
5. Turn stabilization into a growing airborne clock, or retain resources against a sweeper. These are branches, not a guaranteed opening hand.

## 4. Mulligans

A keepable seven usually has two or three lands, reliable early white, a one-mana play, and a productive follow-up. Blue access matters for Quantum Riddler, but a strong white sequence can function without it. Momo, Friendly Flier plus warp plus Daydream is especially attractive.

Ship hands full of expensive creatures without warp support, hands whose lands cannot sustain colored mana, and interaction-only hands without pressure. One-land hands are risky: Springleaf Drum needs both its own mana and a creature, and Starfield Shepherd cannot search until cast. Four-land hands need strong card generation. Against fast aggression, favor lifelink or removal over speculative engine assemblies.

## 5. Combat and defense

Develop flyers before combat when their entries will enlarge an existing, attack-ready Momo, Friendly Flier. A doubled Sage of the Skies gives it +2/+2 that turn. Attack freely over grounded opposition, but weigh instant removal against damage estimates and avoid exchanging essential engines for marginal damage.

Grounded Soldier tokens are useful blockers and Drum fuel. Warped creatures destined to leave can trade profitably, although they cannot normally attack the turn they enter. Preserve Sage of the Skies when repeated lifelink races successfully. Zack Fair can protect a key blocker or attacker for {1}, transferring its counters; indestructible does not answer exile, bounce, or toughness reduction.

## 6. Resources and timing

Leave {W} for Erode or {1}{W} for Get Lost when an opposing threat demands it. Daydream is a sorcery, not protection against removal on the opponent's turn. Opponents can also remove its target in response; losing the target prevents the blink.

A Momo, Friendly Flier discount applies once per turn to the first qualifying spell, not whichever flyer you prefer. Casting Nurturing Pixie first consumes it without reducing {W}. Blinking Momo, Friendly Flier creates a fresh object but removes its accumulated temporary boosts.

Abandoned Air Temple's activation needs {3}{W} plus tapping the Temple: ordinarily five lands' worth of resources. Use it after committing your attackers but before damage, or at the opponent's end step. Warped cards in exile are available only on later turns, at normal creature timing and normal costs, subject to applicable discounts.

## 7. What beats it and how to adapt

Cheap removal on Momo, Friendly Flier or a warp-blink target disrupts tempo. Sequence independent threats when opponents hold mana, and avoid building an entire turn around one exposed creature. Sweepers punish doubled bodies and counters; retain follow-up creatures, Daydream flashback, and warped cards in exile rather than overextending.

Fast aggression pressures Hallowed Fountain payments and slow setup: prioritize Sage of the Skies and cheap answers. Large flyers obstruct the main clock; reserve Get Lost or Erode for them. Artifact/enchantment engines can be answered with Curious Farm Animals for {2} plus sacrificing it. Seam Rip only handles mana value two or less. No sideboard was supplied, so adaptation here means sequencing and resource allocation.

## 8. Common mistakes

- Blinking a Sage of the Skies token: it cannot return. Blinking the card does not repeat its cast trigger.
- Forgetting that Daydream resets counters and attack eligibility rather than stacking counters indefinitely.
- Casting Mockingbird with too little actual mana spent: Momo, Friendly Flier's discount reduces its copy ceiling. Blinking it does not preserve an expensive copied form.
- Casting Guiding Hydra with X zero, or sacrificing its final counter without accepting its death.
- Treating Map tokens or Erode's basic-land compensation as harmless; both can improve the opponent's next turn.
