# Izzet Spellementals

Written 2026-10-11 by gpt-6.1-sol:high from the list at https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/7954745, cards generated 2026-10-10. Sideboardless alpha: main deck only. Advice for the pregame; the board and card text win any disagreement.

## 1. Archetype and game plan

Izzet Spellementals is a spell-heavy tempo deck: filter draws, build the graveyard, then deploy oversized Elementals cheaply. Its 60 cards comprise 20 lands, 12 creatures, and 28 instants/sorceries. The early turns establish resources rather than attack; turns three through five can produce the decisive swing. Eddymurk Crab disrupts combat, while Sunderflock clears non-Elemental blockers and supplies an airborne clock.

Win through repeated creature attacks, with Burst Lightning and Traumatic Critique providing reach. Against aggression, stabilize before racing. Against slower decks, assemble a threat plus protection rather than spending everything on one vulnerable creature.

## 3. The curve and sequencing by turn

Lead Spirebluff Canal while it enters untapped. Island supplies painless early blue. Riverpyre Verge produces blue only with an Island or Mountain; Steam Vents supplies both types, while Spirebluff Canal supplies neither. Choose Island or Mountain for Multiversal Passage according to needed colors and Verge activation. Stormcarved Coast is preferably land three or later; Canal is preferably among the first three lands.

**On the play:**
- **Turn one:** Untapped blue, usually Sleight of Hand to find land two; use Opt when preserving flexibility matters.
- **Turn two:** Prismari Charm's surveil mode advances setup, or leave interaction available against a dangerous play.
- **Turn three:** Winternight Stories can fill the graveyard. Alternatively, combine cheap spells and interaction; cast Flow State once enabled.
- **Turn four:** Deploy a discounted Elemental. If five instant/sorcery cards are already in the graveyard, four blue-capable mana casts Eddymurk Crab for {U}{U}, then Sunderflock for {U}{U}. This is a conditional payoff, not an automatic curve.
- **Turn five:** Attack with surviving threats; add another threat or retain protection rather than automatically tapping out.

**On the draw:**
- **Turn one:** Burst Lightning may take priority over selection against an immediate attacker.
- **Turn two:** Use Impractical Joke or Prismari Charm to contain pressure; otherwise develop the engine.
- **Turn three:** Refill only if the opposing attack is manageable. Cheap removal plus selection often beats a full-turn draw spell.
- **Turn four:** Prioritize a blocker or Sunderflock reset over maximum card velocity.
- **Turn five:** Turn stabilization into attacks while reserving an answer to the opponent's rebuild.

## 4. Mulligans

Keep most hands with two or three lands, access to blue and eventual red, early selection, and either interaction or a credible payoff path. Two lands plus multiple cheap filters is reasonable with only 20 lands. Check actual land sequencing: Riverpyre Verge plus Spirebluff Canal does not provide two blue mana.

Ship hands full of expensive creatures without graveyard development, one-land hands relying on a single selection spell, and land-heavy hands without meaningful action. Against aggression, prioritize removal; against slower decks, selection and a protected threat matter more. A payoff-free hand can work if its filtering is strong, but four Sunderflock without an enabling Elemental is not an engine.

## 5. Combat and defense

Eddymurk Crab cast during your turn enters untapped and can defend immediately. Cast during the opponent's turn, it enters tapped: it cannot ambush-block. To prevent attacks, resolve its tap trigger before attackers are declared. Tapping attackers afterward does not remove them from combat. An end-step Crab threatens an attack next turn, but its tap effect does not prevent the opponent's next normal untap.

Use Crab before blockers to clear a path for established attackers. Sunderflock preserves all your listed creatures while returning non-Elemental creatures, making its precombat deployment especially valuable. None of these creatures has haste.

Attack when the return swing remains survivable. Preserve your only Elemental when it enables a cheap Sunderflock next turn; avoid unnecessary trades or multi-blocks that surrender the engine. When stabilization requires trading, surviving takes priority over discounts. A lone Sunderflock often supplies enough pressure without committing every ground creature.

## 6. Resources and timing

Reserve {U} for Spell Pierce, {U}{U} for Get Out, {U}{R} for Prismari Charm, or {R} for Burst Lightning. These are different shields: Spell Pierce cannot counter creatures, and Get Out counters only creature or enchantment spells. Get Out's return mode rescues your creatures, not opposing ones; retaining another Elemental can make a returned Sunderflock cheap to recast.

Traumatic Critique costs {X}{U}{R}: two mana at X=0 for draw-and-discard with a legal target, or five mana at X=3 to combine damage with replenishment. Targeting the opponent avoids losing the entire spell when a targeted creature disappears. Kicked Burst Lightning costs {4}{R}, five total, for four damage.

Resolve setup before committing discounted threats. Opponents can remove your enabling Elemental before you begin casting Sunderflock, but removal after its cost is paid does not reprice that spell. Likewise, graveyard exile matters most before casting a discounted creature. Budget shock-land life against the damage saved by immediate interaction.

## 7. What beats it and how to adapt

Graveyard exile disables cheap Eddymurk Crab and Hearth Elemental. Establish a creature beforehand when possible; its mana value can still discount Sunderflock even after the graveyard disappears. Keep redundant enablers rather than trusting one exposed creature.

Fast wide boards punish setup turns. Spend removal early and prioritize Sunderflock. Opposing Elementals survive its trigger, while creatures with useful enter-the-battlefield abilities profit from being replayed: bouncing is tempo, not permanent removal.

Countermagic and efficient removal punish concentrated investment. Get Out counters these creature payoffs; Spell Pierce can stop your setup or protection. Stagger threats and retain mana. Prismari Charm answers troublesome nonland permanents temporarily, but the list has no universal permanent-removal solution. Against sweepers, hold additional creatures and rebuild after the opponent commits.

## 8. Common mistakes

- Counting exiled Stoke Genius as a graveyard sorcery, or discarded Hearth Elemental toward Eddymurk Crab.
- Forgetting Winternight Stories leaves the graveyard when harmonized.
- Treating discounted casting cost as mana value.
- Trying to block with an opponent-turn Eddymurk Crab.
- Tapping your sole defender for harmonize without checking the return attack.
- Casting Stoke Genius before using valuable cards or making a needed land drop.
- Playing a fourth-land Spirebluff Canal when untapped mana is essential.
- Assuming Sunderflock returns opposing Elementals, or forgetting its reset requires that it was cast.
