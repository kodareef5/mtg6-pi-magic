# 4c Reanimator

Written 2026-10-11 by gpt-6.1-sol:high from the list at https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/7987028, cards generated 2026-10-10. Sideboardless alpha: main deck only. Advice for the pregame; the board and card text win any disagreement.

## 1. Archetype and game plan

4c Reanimator is a graveyard-combo midrange deck: establish blockers and selection, stock the graveyard, then cast Superior Spider-Man copying Bringer of the Last Gift. That produces a board reset and mass return for four mana rather than eight. Turn four is the natural payoff turn; turns five onward offer better-stocked graveyards and more resilient setups.

Terror of the Peaks converts returning creatures into immediate damage. Ardyn, the Usurper gives your Demons haste, menace, and lifelink, enabling a lethal attack or a major life swing. Without either payoff, the returned army and flying threats can win over subsequent turns. The reset is symmetrical, so compare both graveyards before committing.

## 2. Engine and key interactions

- **Prepare the reset:** Cast Formidable Speaker for {2}{G}, discard Bringer of the Last Gift, and search for Superior Spider-Man. Next turn, cast Superior Spider-Man for {2}{U}{B}, choosing the discarded Bringer. Alternatively, discard another card to find Bringer, then discard it through Oblivious Bookworm or Seasoned Cryomancer before casting Superior Spider-Man.
- **Resolve deliberately:** Superior Spider-Man enters as a 4/4 with Bringer's abilities. Because you cast it, the reset condition is satisfied. For a straightforward sequence, put the copied reset trigger below the Mind Swap exile trigger: exile the copied card first, then sacrifice all other creatures and return eligible graveyard creatures. Creatures sacrificed by this resolution do not immediately return. A Bringer returned without being cast does not reset the board again.
- **Stock and select:** Cache Grab costs {1}{G} and mills four; Rapid Rescue costs {G} and mills two. Keep a needed land or setup permanent, but usually leave payoff creatures in the graveyard. Overlord of the Balemurk, cast for its {1}{B} impending cost, mills four and can recover a non-Avatar creature from anywhere in your graveyard. Recover Superior Spider-Man rather than automatically retrieving a reanimation target.
- **Convert the return:** Terror of the Peaks sees other creatures entering simultaneously with it. One returning Terror alongside two returning Bringer of the Last Gift and Ardyn, the Usurper produces 6 + 6 + 4 = 16 damage before combat. The already-present Superior Spider-Man contributes no entry damage. Ardyn grants haste to both returned Bringers and a Superior Spider-Man copying Bringer because they are Demons. A naturally returned Terror is a Dragon, not a Demon.
- **Alternative copies:** Superior Spider-Man can copy Ardyn for its combat engine or Terror for future entry damage. Copying Deceit and paying {U}{U}{B}{B} for Superior Spider-Man satisfies both mana-spent conditions, yielding bounce and discard without evoke sacrifice. Copying Wistfulness can give its draw/discard effect with two blue spent, or artifact/enchantment exile with two green spent; the normal four-mana Spider cost cannot supply both pairs while also paying its required black.

Formidable Speaker's {1}, {T} ability can reuse a land for color fixing or untap a blocker. Paying one mana to untap a normal one-mana land is not ramp, and a newly cast Speaker cannot activate it immediately.

## 3. The curve and sequencing by turn

**On the play:**

1. Lead a shock land tapped when there is no immediate play, or an untapped green source for Rapid Rescue if selection is needed. Preserve Starting Town's early untapped window.
2. Cast Oblivious Bookworm with {G}{U} for repeated end-step filtering, Overlord of the Balemurk for {1}{B}, or Cache Grab for {1}{G} when finding land three matters most.
3. Cast Formidable Speaker to assemble Bringer plus Superior Spider-Man, or Seasoned Cryomancer for {1}{U}{U} to dig and discard. Discarding nonlands also freezes opposing creatures.
4. Cast Superior Spider-Man for {2}{U}{B} if the reset improves the position. Otherwise continue setup rather than returning a stronger opposing graveyard.
5. Exploit the rebuilt board, remove a blocker, or deploy another value threat. Five lands allow Superior Spider-Man plus Rapid Rescue; cast Rescue first if it may improve the available copy or return targets.

**On the draw:** The same curve applies, but survival takes priority. On turn two, evoked Deceit with {U}{U} can bounce a threat, while {B}{B} attacks the opponent's hand. On turn three, Seasoned Cryomancer's stun counters often buy more time than a tutor. On turn four, stabilize even with a modest return if waiting risks lethal damage. Turn five becomes recovery rather than mandatory aggression.

Lead typed lands to enable Wastewood Verge and Willowrush Verge. Both always provide their first listed color; their second colors require the relevant land types. Arrange double blue for Seasoned Cryomancer and blue-black for Superior Spider-Man. Starting Town enters tapped from your fourth turn onward.

## 4. Mulligans

The list contains 23 lands, 30 creature cards, and seven other spells. A keepable seven usually has two or three lands, usable green/blue access, an early selection spell or blocker, and a credible path toward the engine. Two lands plus Cache Grab or Rapid Rescue is much safer than two lands plus only expensive creatures.

Ship hands dominated by eight-mana threats, one-land hands without a credible recovery plan, and hands whose apparent colors depend entirely on unsuitable Cavern of Souls choices. Four lands plus setup and a payoff can be acceptable; five lands without selection usually lacks action. On the draw against aggression, prioritize a functional early defense over a perfect combo hand.

## 5. Combat and defense

Oblivious Bookworm and Formidable Speaker are useful early blockers. Preserve them when their filtering or tutoring matters, but trade them when necessary to survive: creatures traded before the reset can return, whereas creatures sacrificed by the reset cannot.

Attack early only when leaving blockers back is unnecessary. After a reset, count immediate Terror damage and Demon combat damage separately; returned creatures without haste cannot attack that turn. Ardyn's menace requires two blockers per attacking Demon, while lifelink can make an otherwise nonlethal attack stabilize the game.

Seasoned Cryomancer buys attack windows as well as defensive time. Use its nonland discards to stun the creatures that would otherwise block your decisive attack or deal the most damage next turn.

## 6. Resources and timing

This is primarily a tap-out deck, not a counterspell deck. Hold {1}{G} for Cache Grab when no immediate development is required, or {G} for Rapid Rescue. Awaken the Honored Dead costs {B}{G}{U}; its first chapter supplies broad removal, but Cavern of Souls cannot provide colored mana for it.

Cavern of Souls naming Human supports Oblivious Bookworm, Seasoned Cryomancer, Superior Spider-Man, and Ardyn, the Usurper. Naming Dragon helps cast Terror of the Peaks. The only red-capable lands are three Starting Town and three Cavern of Souls: six conditional sources, with Cavern requiring Dragon for Terror. Hard-casting Terror's {3}{R}{R} is therefore a backup, not the default plan.

Use end-step selection to minimize information given away. Seasoned Cryomancer's graveyard ability costs {3}{U}{U} and exiles it: valuable in stalled games, but it consumes a potential returning creature. Overlord's impending counters tick down at your end steps, including the turn it enters; while still noncreature, it survives Bringer's creature sacrifice.

## 7. What beats it and how to adapt

Graveyard exile and replacement effects disrupt both selection and the reset payoff. Use Awaken the Honored Dead or Wistfulness against appropriate permanent-based hate before committing more resources. Otherwise pivot toward ordinary creatures, card selection, and eventual hard casts.

Fast aggression punishes shock-land payments, Starting Town life payments, and setup turns. Favor blockers, Rapid Rescue's two life, and Cryomancer stuns. Countermagic punishes the payoff spell; Cavern of Souls naming Human protects Superior Spider-Man, but does not protect its triggered abilities from applicable interaction.

Opposing graveyard decks can profit from your reset. Deceit's discard can accidentally improve their return pool. Inspect both graveyards, use Ardyn's combat trigger to exile a key opposing creature when possible, and decline the reset when ordinary value is safer.

## 8. Common mistakes

- Treating a returned Bringer as another reset, or expecting creatures sacrificed by that reset to return immediately.
- Forgetting that Oblivious Bookworm normally requires a discard: this list has no native face-down enabler.
- Expecting evoked Deceit paid with {U}{B} to trigger either two-of-one-color ability, or expecting uncast returned Incarnations to remember earlier mana payments.
- Assuming Cache Grab produces Food: this list contains no Squirrels.
- Spending Cavern's restricted colored mana on selection spells, or ignoring Starting Town's turn-four tapped entry.
- Counting non-Demons as hasty, omitting opposing returns, or sacrificing an established Terror and expecting it to return in the same reset.
