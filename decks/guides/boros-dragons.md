# Boros Dragons

Written 2026-10-11 by gpt-6.1-sol:high from the list at https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/7974167, cards generated 2026-10-10. Sideboardless alpha: main deck only. Advice for the pregame; the board and card text win any disagreement.

## 1. Archetype and game plan

Boros Dragons is proactive flying midrange with tempo-disruption tools. Its 60 cards comprise 24 lands, 30 creatures, and six dedicated instants; two creatures also offer Charring Bite as removal. Momo, Friendly Flier and Sarkhan, Dragon Ascendant accelerate Dragons, while Voice of Victory and Clarion Conqueror restrict opposing interaction.

The deck aims to deploy meaningful Dragons on turn three, then win through repeated aerial attacks, haste damage, and Smaug the Magnificent's attack trigger. Magmatic Hellkite delays opposing mana development while adding a substantial threat. This is not pure ramp: spending every Treasure immediately can weaken Smaug, and deploying Clarion Conqueror can disable your own acceleration.

## 2. Engine and key interactions

- **Momo discounts:** With Momo, Friendly Flier present, your first non-Lemur flying creature spell each turn costs one generic mana less. Clarion Conqueror costs {1}{W}; Magmatic Hellkite and Smaug the Magnificent cost {1}{R}{R}; Nova Hellkite costs {2}{R}{R} normally or {1}{R} through warp; Twinmaw Stormbrood costs {4}{W}. Colored requirements remain unchanged. A second qualifying spell receives no discount, even if the first was countered.
- **Sarkhan acceleration:** Cast Sarkhan, Dragon Ascendant for {1}{R}, then behold a Dragon when its entry trigger resolves to make a Treasure. Revealing a Dragon does not spend that card. With three lands plus that Treasure, a four-mana Dragon is available on turn three without Momo. With Momo, three lands plus Treasure can instead cast a full-cost Nova Hellkite for its reduced four-mana cost.
- **Growing attackers:** Each Dragon entering gives Sarkhan a permanent +1/+1 counter and temporary flying. Cast Dragons before combat when you want an established Sarkhan to attack in the air. Each other flying creature entering also pumps Momo for that turn. Neither effect gives a newly cast Sarkhan haste.
- **Treasure tension:** Smaug the Magnificent creates a Treasure at each of your upkeeps, not when he enters. His attack trigger counts your Treasures when it resolves. Keep them through resolution to maximize damage. Clarion Conqueror prevents all artifact, creature, and planeswalker activated abilities, including your Treasure mana abilities. Spend needed Treasures before it resolves; it does not prevent Treasure creation, Smaug's triggers, or ordinary land abilities.
- **Warp reuse:** Nova Hellkite can enter for {2}{R}, or {1}{R} with Momo, ping an opposing creature, and attack immediately. Its warp trigger exiles it at the next end step. On a later turn, cast it from exile for {3}{R}{R}, or {2}{R}{R} with Momo's available discount. Warp itself is available only from hand. If Nova dies before the exile trigger resolves, that line does not preserve it.
- **Removal modes:** Charring Bite costs {1}{R}, deals five to a creature without flying, then shuffles Twinmaw Stormbrood into its owner's library; it does not reserve a creature to cast later. Momo does not discount this sorcery. Burst Lightning costs {R} for two damage or {4}{R} for four. Erode costs {W}, but can give the opponent a tapped basic land.

## 3. The curve and sequencing by turn

**On the play, with acceleration:**

1. Lead an untapped white source and cast Momo, Friendly Flier for {W}. Prefer Inspiring Vantage over paying life when possible.
2. Establish red access and cast Sarkhan, Dragon Ascendant for {1}{R}, revealing a Dragon for Treasure. Alternatively, cast discounted Clarion Conqueror for {1}{W} when shutting down opposing activations matters more than ramp.
3. With Momo and Sarkhan, cast Smaug the Magnificent or Magmatic Hellkite for {1}{R}{R}, retaining Treasure if possible. Smaug provides immediate haste pressure; Magmatic Hellkite attacks a nonbasic land and prepares next turn's assault.
4. Use Smaug's upkeep Treasure, if present, to support another Dragon or removal. A normal Nova Hellkite costs {2}{R}{R} with Momo. Attack with established creatures after resolving useful entry triggers.
5. Continue presenting lethal pressure, but preserve removal mana if a blocker would stop the attack. Twinmaw Stormbrood becomes available for {4}{W} with Momo when its five life matters more than haste.

**On the draw:** Turn one may instead require Burst Lightning for {R}; without Momo, turn-two Sarkhan can still enable a turn-three four-mana Dragon using Treasure. Voice of Victory for {1}{W} is another turn-two option, but supplies protection rather than acceleration. Turn three may need Charring Bite or Erode instead of a threat. Turns four and five usually favor stabilizing with a 4/5 flier before racing.

Play Inspiring Vantage among your first three lands; as the fourth land, it normally enters tapped. Sacred Foundry, Multiversal Passage, Mountain, and Plains can enable Sunbillow Verge's red ability: there are 4 + 4 + 1 + 1 = 10 such cards. Choose Passage's type deliberately. Sunbillow Verge always supplies white, but supplies red only with a Mountain or Plains. Default Cavern of Souls to Dragon unless the immediate hand requires another type.

## 4. Mulligans

Keep most sevens with two or three lands, usable early colors, an early creature or removal spell, and a Dragon payoff. Two-land Momo hands need a realistic path to red; two-land Sarkhan hands want a Dragon to behold. Three lands, Sarkhan, a four-mana Dragon, and interaction form a sound keep without Momo.

Ship hands dominated by expensive Dragons without early plays, one-land hands dependent on several draws, and hands whose lands cannot cast their supposed curve. Cavern of Souls and Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon are not unrestricted colored sources. A four-land seven needs meaningful action; five lands and little pressure usually warrants a mulligan.

## 5. Combat and defense

Cast entry-trigger Dragons before combat to increase Momo's power and give an established Sarkhan flying. Nova Hellkite's one damage can clear a small blocker before attackers are declared. Smaug's attack trigger can remove a blocker before blocks or finish the opponent directly.

Attack Voice of Victory when two temporary Warriors justify exposing the 1/3. The Warriors enter tapped and attacking, then are sacrificed at the next end step; they are not future blockers. Their entry does not itself trigger abilities requiring a creature to attack.

Against aggressive decks, hold a 4/5 Dragon back when one block prevents more damage than your attack threatens. Trade Sarkhan when survival demands it, but recognize its growing value. Preserve Momo when its discount enables your next Dragon. Avoid sacrificing Voice merely for two damage if its spell restriction protects a decisive following turn.

## 6. Resources and timing

Voice of Victory prevents opponents casting spells during your turn, but not activating abilities. Clarion Conqueror covers many activations, but neither card stops triggered abilities. Together they constrain interaction without making combat invulnerable. Opponents can still remove these creatures during their own turns.

Reserve {R} for Burst Lightning or {W} for Erode when an opposing threat warrants it. Cavern of Souls' colored mana cannot pay either instant; Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon's colored mana can pay Charring Bite because it is an Omen spell. Both lands can contribute colorless mana toward generic costs.

Magmatic Hellkite's replacement basic enters tapped with a stun counter, usually missing its controller's next untap before becoming available at the following one. Erode accelerates the opponent without that stun delay. Use this distinction when planning the race.

Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon's search requires four mana from elsewhere, tapping and sacrificing the land itself. It finds a Dragon into hand, not onto the battlefield: reserve it for stalled games rather than sacrificing development prematurely.

## 7. What beats it and how to adapt

Cheap removal on Momo or Sarkhan disrupts the accelerated curve. Opposing Burst Lightning is particularly effective before Sarkhan grows. Against removal-heavy decks, favor hands that function without acceleration and use Cavern of Souls to protect Dragon spells from counters.

Sweepers punish committing every creature. Keep a follow-up Dragon, especially a warped Nova Hellkite in exile, rather than adding unnecessary power to an already threatening board. Voice of Victory does not stop a sweeper cast on the opponent's turn.

Fast ground aggression punishes life payments and slow hands. Prioritize early removal, defensive Dragons, and Twinmaw Stormbrood's life gain. Large flying blockers require Erode, Smaug damage, or combined burn; Charring Bite cannot target them. Basic-heavy mana bases reduce Magmatic Hellkite's disruption. No sideboard is supplied, so adaptations here are sequencing and mulligan decisions.

## 8. Common mistakes

- Planning to sacrifice Treasures after Clarion Conqueror resolves.
- Spending Smaug's Treasures before his damage trigger resolves without accounting for reduced damage.
- Assuming Momo discounts every flier or reduces colored costs.
- Treating warped Nova Hellkite as immediately recastable, or expecting warp from exile.
- Using restricted colored land mana for removal it cannot pay for.
- Attacking Sarkhan before resolving the Dragon entry trigger that grants flying.
- Treating Voice of Victory as protection from abilities or opposing-turn removal.
- Casting Erode casually when its extra land restores the opponent's disrupted curve.
