# Lifegain

Written 2026-10-11 by gpt-6.1-sol:high from the list at https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/7991954, cards generated 2026-10-10. Sideboardless alpha: main deck only. Advice for the pregame; the board and card text win any disagreement.

## 1. Archetype and game plan

Lifegain is a white-black creature-engine midrange deck: 22 lands and 38 nonlands. It establishes cheap life-gain sources, then turns creature entries and combat into counters, cards, recursion, and life loss. It is not a deterministic combo deck. Turns one through three build the engine; subsequent turns overwhelm opponents through repeated triggers and resilient threats.

Amalia Benavides Aguirre supplies selection and a growing attacker. Starscape Cleric converts each separate life-gain event into damage-like life loss; Lunar Convocation adds attrition and flying tokens. Ajani Resolute supplies growing tokens or an eventual anthem emblem. Flying attacks and accumulated drain usually finish games before Amalia's exactly-20-power destruction becomes necessary.

## 2. Engine and key interactions

- **Entry multiplication:** Case of the Uneaten Feast, Hinterland Sanctifier, Liliana the Faultless, and Haliya, Guided by Light each produce separate gains. With all four present, another creature entering generates four separate 1-life events: four Amalia explores, four Ajani loyalty counters, and four Starscape Cleric triggers. Case and Haliya trigger on their own creature entries; Hinterland Sanctifier and Liliana do not trigger on themselves.
- **Cheap acceleration:** Turn-one Hinterland Sanctifier for {W}, turn-two Amalia Benavides Aguirre for {W}{B} gives one immediate explore. On turn three, cast Case of the Uneaten Feast for {W}, then Enlightened Confidant for {1}{W}: its entry produces two gains and two explores, with lifelink threatening more.
- **Burst turns:** Moseo, Vein's New Dean costs {2}{B} and brings a Pest. With Case plus Hinterland Sanctifier, its two creature entries yield 2 × 2 = 4 life. Add Haliya and that becomes six, enough for Haliya's draw, Case's solve condition, and recursion of any creature in this list through Moseo. The Pest gains life when declared attacking, before blockers.
- **Paying life profitably:** Lunar Convocation costs {W}{B}; its draw activation costs {1}{B} plus 2 life. Starting Town's colored mana or Godless Shrine's untapped entry can also establish life loss. Gain and loss are tracked separately, not netted, so gaining 1 and paying 2 still enables Convocation's Bat condition.
- **Recursion:** Moseo returns creatures without a mana payment. Zoraline, Cosmos Caller costs {1}{W}{B}, then each entry/attack recursion requires another {W}{B} and 2 life; it can return cheap nonland permanents, including Case or Ajani. Finality counters exile those permanents instead of letting them return to the graveyard. A solved Case grants graveyard casting, not free casting: sacrifice it during a main phase and budget normal spell costs.
- **Emptiness:** Evoke for {W}{W} to reanimate a creature of mana value three or less, or {B}{B} to place three -1/-1 counters. Evoking for {W}{B} enables neither effect. At six mana, spending at least two white and two black enables both. Order its sacrifice above its other entry triggers when you want it in the graveyard first; returning Emptiness without casting it does not satisfy either spent-mana condition.

## 3. The curve and sequencing by turn

The printed curve is 13 one-mana, 17 two-mana, seven three-mana, and one six-mana spell. Warp and evoke provide cheaper alternatives; Starscape Cleric with offspring costs {3}{B}{B}, five mana total.

**On the play:** Turn one, lead Concealed Courtyard, Plains, or untapped Godless Shrine into Hinterland Sanctifier, Liliana the Faultless, or Case. Turn two, establish Amalia or another payoff; use Deep-Cavern Bat instead when stripping an answer protects the following turn. Turn three, deploy Moseo or combine a one-drop with a two-drop. Turn four, develop while reserving {W} for Erode when required. Turn five, pursue recursion, an offspring Cleric, or multiple cheap spells rather than automatically choosing the most expensive play.

**On the draw:** Keep the same opening engine plan, but prioritize Deep-Cavern Bat or Erode on turn two against pressure. Turn three may require removal plus a two-drop rather than a three-drop. Turns four and five should stabilize first, then exploit lifelink attacks and recursion once blocks are safe.

Lead Shattered Sanctum only when no turn-one spell matters; it becomes untapped with two other lands. Concealed Courtyard wants an early land drop. Starting Town is untapped only during your first three turns, not your first three land drops. Bleachbone Verge produces white only with a Plains or Swamp, including Godless Shrine. Dalkovan Encampment wants a Swamp already present.

## 4. Mulligans

Keep two or three lands with reliable early white, black by turn two or three, a cheap gain source, and a payoff or interaction. Three lands, Hinterland Sanctifier, Amalia, Erode, and a follow-up is a coherent seven.

Ship hands that cannot cast their early spells, land-heavy hands without an engine, and one-land hands dependent on drawing perfectly. Amalia finds lands only after gaining life; she does not repair an otherwise nonfunctional hand. Avoid keeping several legendary duplicates without development or interaction. Against fast decks, prioritize immediate board presence over Lunar Convocation alone.

## 5. Combat and defense

Attack when lifelink, Pest triggers, or Bat triggers cross a useful threshold without sacrificing essential engines. Enlightened Confidant and Deep-Cavern Bat gain life through damage; blocked or removed attackers may fail to deliver it. Zoraline gains life separately for each attacking Bat, and vigilance preserves her defense. Moseo is a Bird, not a Bat.

Starscape Cleric cannot block, including its offspring token. Preserve Hinterland Sanctifier and Liliana when their future triggers exceed a trade's value, but trade expendable bodies to protect Ajani or survive. Deep-Cavern Bat dying returns its exiled card, so evaluate that cost before blocking.

Amalia can attack aggressively as she grows, but reaching exactly 20 destroys every other creature, including your engines. It does not destroy planeswalkers or enchantments. This is a tactical reset, not an automatic win.

## 6. Resources and timing

Reserve {W} for Erode against decisive threats, accepting that its controller may receive a tapped basic land. Liliana's protection needs {1}, tapping her, and discarding a card; she must be able to tap, cannot protect herself, and does not answer untargeted sweepers.

Warp Haliya for {W} to obtain a cheap burst, then cast her from exile on a later turn for {2}{W}. Her draw trigger can resolve before the warp exile trigger. Unlike Convocation's conditional end-step triggers, Haliya checks the three-life requirement when her draw resolves: end-step gains can help her.

Order end-step triggers deliberately. Moseo's returned creature or Convocation's Bat can generate more gains before Haliya draws or Enlightened Confidant surveils. Confidant can recover the surveilled card only if its mana value fits that turn's gained-life total. Choose Moseo's legal target when putting its trigger on the stack; later gains cannot retroactively choose a different target. Do not wait until the end step to establish a condition needed for a trigger to begin.

Dalkovan Encampment's activation costs {2}{W} and taps the land: normally four lands' worth of resources. Activate before attacking. Its entering Warriors trigger your entry engines even though they were not declared as attackers.

## 7. What beats it and how to adapt

Sweepers, exile removal, graveyard hate, and effects preventing life gain attack the deck's infrastructure. Fast flyers exploit small ground defenders and nonblocking Clerics; larger threats can overwhelm the two Erode copies. Control punishes overextension and expensive recursion commitments.

Use Deep-Cavern Bat before committing key engines. Spread value across enchantments, Ajani, and creatures rather than exposing everything to creature destruction. Hold a creature to restart after a sweeper; retain a solved Case for a later rebuilding main phase. Against graveyard hate, shift toward attacks, Cleric drain, and Convocation cards instead of relying on recursion. Against aggression, spend removal early and preserve actual blockers.

## 8. Common mistakes

- Counting life gained rather than separate gain events for Amalia, Ajani, and Cleric triggers.
- Treating Amalia's destruction as "20 or more," or assuming explores always grow her. Lands go to hand; retained nonlands can be revealed repeatedly.
- Activating two Ajani loyalty abilities in one turn. His 0 gains one life and thus normally one loyalty; using −4 at exactly four loyalty puts him into the graveyard before token-entry gains can rescue him.
- Sacrificing a newly solved Case in the end step expecting normal creature casts then.
- Forgetting summoning sickness, legendary duplication, finality counters, or the mana still required for graveyard casting.
- Missing Convocation's life-loss requirement or sacrificing necessary defense for an insignificant gain trigger.
