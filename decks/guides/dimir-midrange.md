# Dimir Midrange

Written 2026-10-11 by gpt-6.1-sol:high from the list at https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/7954750, cards generated 2026-10-10. Sideboardless alpha: main deck only. Advice for the pregame; the board and card text win any disagreement.

## 1. Archetype and game plan

Dimir Midrange is an evasive tempo deck that turns early combat connections into lasting card advantage. Its nine one-mana creatures, 4 Dream Beavers + 4 Spyglass Siren + 1 Cecil, Dark Knight, establish pressure; cheap interaction keeps opponents from stabilizing. It usually wins through repeated attacks, not one explosive turn.

Kaito, Bane of Nightmares rewards an unblocked attacker, while Enduring Curiosity makes each connecting creature another card. Once either engine works, prioritize maintaining profitable attacks over deploying every threat. Against aggression, trade resources first and become the attacker after stabilizing. Against slower decks, establish a clock while reserving protection. Restless Reef and Soulstone Sanctuary provide threats without spending additional creature cards.

## 2. Engine and key interactions

- **The Kaito line:** Cast Spyglass Siren for {U} or Dream Beavers for {B}. On a later turn, attack and wait for blockers. Return an unblocked attacker and pay {1}{U}{B} to put Kaito, Bane of Nightmares onto the battlefield tapped and attacking. Before damage, this replaces a small attacker with a 3/4. In your second main phase, choose an emblem for future pressure or use 0 to surveil and draw if the opponent lost life. Returning the one-drop also prepares another Map or drain-and-scry trigger when you recast it for one mana.
- **Damage first is another line:** You can let the original attacker connect, then use ninjutsu while it remains unblocked during combat. With Enduring Curiosity, this preserves that attacker's draw trigger; Kaito does not deal additional combat damage afterward. Dream Beavers can also enable Kaito's 0 through its life-loss trigger without a combat connection.
- **Curiosity conversion:** Pay {2}{U}{U} on the opponent's end step to untap with Enduring Curiosity, or flash it in before your creatures deal combat damage. Three connecting creatures mean three draw triggers, not one. Its death return preserves the draw engine as a noncreature enchantment, but exile or bounce avoids that return.
- **Clear the lane:** Floodpits Drowner costs {1}{U}; flash it in to tap a blocker and add a stun counter. Once it can activate its tap ability, pay another {1}{U} and tap it to shuffle itself and a stunned creature away. Kaito's −2 supplies two stun counters and can prepare this line. A stun counter replaces an untap with removing one counter; it does not fall off merely because a turn passes.
- **Grow and filter:** Spend {1} at sorcery speed to use Spyglass Siren's Map. Explore either supplies a land or grows the creature and filters a nonland. Soulstone Sanctuary's {4} animation persists and gives it all creature types, including Ninja, so Kaito's emblems increase its size.

## 3. The curve and sequencing by turn

**On the play:** Turn one, deploy a flyer. Turn two, attack and keep up {1}{U} for Floodpits Drowner or We Say Thee Nay!, or spend spare mana on a Map. Turn three, use ninjutsu if the attack is safe; otherwise hold Tishana's Tidebinder for {2}{U}. Turn four, develop or flash in Enduring Curiosity, but do not tap out into an obvious punish. Turn five, connect for cards, replay a returned one-drop, and reserve interaction rather than automatically adding another four-drop.

**On the draw:** Turn one still wants a creature, unless stopping the opponent's start is more important. Turn two often belongs to Requiting Hex or two-mana removal. Turn three, favor flash interaction over forcing Kaito through a crowded board. Turn four, stabilize with removal plus a cheap threat, or deploy Enduring Curiosity if safe. Turn five, transition to drawing through attacks once blockers are controlled.

Lead Island or Swamp when possible. Watery Grave enables Gloomlake Verge's black mode but is not a basic land for Hidden Lair. Hidden Lair produces colored mana on its entry turn; afterward it needs an actual basic land. Fit tapped Restless Reef into a turn with spare mana. Avoid early Soulstone Sanctuary when colored requirements are tight, especially {U}{U} on turn four.

## 4. Mulligans

Keep most sevens with two or three functional lands, an early creature, and interaction or a credible engine. Check colors and land conditions, not just the land count: the deck has 24 lands, but two produce only colorless mana, and others have restrictions.

Ship hands whose first meaningful action is turn four, hands dominated by redundant Kaito, Bane of Nightmares without an attacker, and conditional-land hands that cannot reliably cast their spells. A one-land hand is risky even with scry or a Map: neither guarantees the second land, and the Map itself costs mana. Against aggression, require early defense; against control, favor pressure plus protection.

## 5. Combat and defense

Attack with flyers when they can connect safely, especially with Enduring Curiosity active. Preserve them when an opposing flyer would simply trade and your engine depends on their survival. Tapping a blocker after blocks does not make the blocked attacker unblocked.

Floodpits Drowner's vigilance lets it attack and remain available for blocking, teamwork, or its shuffle ability. Cecil, Dark Knight can trade with a large creature through deathtouch, but damage it deals also costs you life. At ten life or less in a normal twenty-life game, its damage trigger can transform it; lethal life loss still ends the game before a rescue.

Cecil, Redeemed Paladin protects other attacking creatures, not itself. Kaito is a creature with hexproof during your turn while it has loyalty, but an opposing attack can pressure it as a planeswalker on theirs. Keep defenders when its survival matters more than another point of damage.

## 6. Resources and timing

Represent answers deliberately: {U} for Spell Pierce or Spell Snare; {B} for Requiting Hex; {1}{B} for Bitter Triumph or Shoot the Sheriff; {1}{U} for We Say Thee Nay! or Floodpits Drowner; {2}{U} for Tishana's Tidebinder; and {2}{U}{U} for Enduring Curiosity.

Teamwork does not reduce We Say Thee Nay!'s mana cost. It taps untapped creatures totaling at least two power to raise the payment from two to four; newly entered creatures can contribute. Consider the lost blockers before committing them.

Use Tishana's Tidebinder while the relevant nonmana ability is on the stack. Its ability suppression lasts only while it remains. The Wondrous Wasp can suppress a creature without waiting for an ability, but removing it restores those abilities.

Bitter Triumph trades either a card or three life for flexibility. Requiting Hex's optional blight can kill your own 1/1; do not pay it automatically. Wan Shi Tong, Librarian costs four mana at X=2 for a 3/3 and one card, or six at X=4 for a 5/5 and two cards. Restless Reef needs four activation mana plus the land itself untapped to attack.

## 7. What beats it and how to adapt

Wide aggressive boards overload single-target answers and attack the life spent on Watery Grave, Bitter Triumph, and Cecil, Dark Knight. Trade early, prioritize dangerous attackers, and avoid speculative shock-land payments.

Flying or reach blockers shut down the easiest engine connections. Tap or remove the key blocker rather than sacrificing flyers into it. Shoot the Sheriff cannot destroy outlaws; stripping abilities with The Wondrous Wasp does not remove creature types.

Sweepers, exile, and bounce punish overdevelopment. Against them, stagger creatures, hold flash threats, and reserve Spell Pierce or We Say Thee Nay! when applicable. Enduring Curiosity survives ordinary destruction differently from exile; animated lands can also become vulnerable to creature removal. No sideboard was supplied, so adapt through sequencing rather than assumed substitutions.

## 8. Common mistakes

- Activating Floodpits Drowner's tap ability before it has overcome summoning sickness.
- Using Kaito's loyalty abilities during combat rather than a main phase with an empty stack.
- Assuming Gloomlake Verge enables itself, or Watery Grave enables an older Hidden Lair.
- Spending both blue sources before planning Enduring Curiosity.
- Treating Spell Pierce as a creature counter or Shoot the Sheriff as unrestricted removal.
- Animating a land played this turn and expecting it to attack, or tapping that land to help pay its activation and expecting an attack.
- Overcommitting creatures instead of protecting the engine already producing cards.
