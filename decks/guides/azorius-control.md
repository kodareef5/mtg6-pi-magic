# Azorius Control

Written 2026-10-11 by gpt-6.1-sol:high from the list at https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/7990810, cards generated 2026-10-10. Sideboardless alpha: main deck only. Advice for the pregame; the board and card text win any disagreement.

## 1. Archetype and game plan

Azorius Control trades early interaction for time, stabilizes with sweepers, then wins through planeswalker-generated creatures and Restless Anchorage. It normally establishes control around turns four through six rather than racing. The Theorist, Jace Beleren supplies repeated cards and blockers; its ultimate turns a stocked hand into a lethal army. Pinnacle Starcage can provide a late Robot army instead.

The deck has 26 lands and no creature cards. Its nine counterspells are four Countersculpt, three No More Lies, one Spell Snare, and one Three Steps Ahead. Against it, pressure both life and loyalty, and force interaction before presenting your most important threat.

## 2. Engine and key interactions

- **Establish Jace cheaply.** Countersculpt costs {U}{U} when you behold a Jace, including revealing The Theorist, Jace Beleren from hand; otherwise it costs {1}{U}{U}. Its empower creates or grows a Jace token. That token enables subsequent behold payments and has separate loyalty abilities from The Theorist, Jace Beleren. Spending its only counter on surveil loses the token and that discount.
- **Convert loyalty into cards.** Jace's Machinations costs {2}{U}, adds eight loyalty only to a Jace token, and allows instant-speed loyalty activation that turn. A fresh token can immediately use −3 to draw, retaining five loyalty. Each planeswalker still gets only one loyalty activation per turn. Casting it on the opponent's turn permits another activation after one on your turn, not two on that same opponent's turn.
- **Grow without spending cards.** Theorist's Sanctum costs {2}{U} plus tapping itself to empower two: budget four available lands. Fatehold Charm's draw mode costs {W}{U} and adds two token loyalty. Neither boosts the nontoken planeswalker.
- **Build the finisher.** The Theorist, Jace Beleren costs {2}{U}{U}, starts at three loyalty, and reaches four with +1. Continue making Illusions when defense allows. Once it has six loyalty, Jace's Machinations can enable its −6 during an opponent's turn, provided it has not already activated that turn. The ultimate counts your hand after drawing three. It grants counters, not haste.
- **Find the right answer.** Consult the Star Charts costs {1}{U}, or {2}{U}{U} kicked. Its selection depth uses lands controlled on resolution. Hold it until the opponent passes when interaction may be necessary.

## 3. The curve and sequencing by turn

**On the play:**

1. Lead Deserted Beach or Restless Anchorage when no one-mana answer is needed. Hallowed Fountain can enter tapped to establish land types without losing life. For immediate interaction, use an appropriate basic, an untapped Hallowed Fountain, or Theorist's Sanctum with a Jace to behold.
2. Establish both colors and hold No More Lies, Get Lost, or discounted Countersculpt. Use Consult the Star Charts only if the opponent offers no worthwhile target. Floodfarm Verge supplies blue only with a Plains or Island; a first-turn Deserted Beach does not enable it.
3. Deserted Beach now enters untapped with two other lands. Hold three-mana interaction or deploy Pinnacle Starcage against a wide cheap board. Use Seam Rip plus a two-mana answer when colors permit.
4. Choose Day of Judgment for stabilization or The Theorist, Jace Beleren for a protected advantage engine. Do not automatically tap out into a dangerous follow-up.
5. With Jace established, make a blocker or bounce a key threat and preserve interaction. Otherwise, deploy it with {W} available for Erode, or wait until six lands to retain a two-mana counter.

**On the draw:** prioritize untapped white and early removal. Turn two often answers an existing threat rather than holding a counter. Turn three Pinnacle Starcage and turn four Day of Judgment become more important; turn five is frequently the first safe planeswalker window. The extra card helps resources but does not compensate for missed defensive mana.

## 4. Mulligans

Keep most three- or four-land hands with functioning white and blue, early interaction, and selection or a payoff. Two lands can work with Consult the Star Charts and cheap defense, but verify that both lands actually support the turn-two play.

Ship one-land hands, colorless-heavy hands, and hands whose first two lands enter tapped against aggression. Four expensive engine spells without early interaction are not a defensive hand. Five lands needs meaningful action and replenishment, not merely a planeswalker. Evaluate Countersculpt at three mana unless a Jace is available to behold.

## 5. Combat and defense

Illusions should usually protect Jace rather than chip in for one damage. Trade or chump when doing so buys another opponent-draw-step trigger or prevents lethal. Before a planned Day of Judgment, expend disposable blockers freely. Tokens without mana costs are vulnerable to Pinnacle Starcage too.

Attack with Restless Anchorage when the opponent cannot profitably remove it or punish your reduced interaction. Animation costs {1}{W}{U}; attacking normally requires three other mana sources so Anchorage stays untapped. A newly played Anchorage cannot attack that turn. Its Map can later improve a creature or become Fountainport fuel.

Fatehold Charm's +1/+2 mode can improve blocks or protect creatures from damage, but not destruction. Under Jace's Machinations, The Theorist, Jace Beleren can bounce an attacker before blockers or create an untapped blocker; respect the once-per-turn activation limit.

## 6. Resources and timing

Prioritize {U}{U} for Countersculpt and {W}{W} for both sweepers. Three Petrified Hamlet plus one Fountainport means four lands produce only colorless intrinsically. Use those for generic costs without compromising colored answers.

Three Steps Ahead costs {1}{U}{U} to counter, {2}{U} to draw two and discard one, or {3}{U} to copy. Counter plus draw/discard costs {3}{U}{U}. Do not treat its printed {U} as the complete price.

Fountainport's token conversion costs {2} plus tapping Fountainport and sacrificing a token. Spend excess Maps or expendable creatures when safe; making a Fish costs {3}, tapping it, and one life. These abilities compete with counterspell mana.

The Theorist, Jace Beleren's trigger occurs each opponent's draw step: the opponent draws normally before you can act. Casting Jace's Machinations in upkeep can make an Illusion before combat, but you cannot activate the same planeswalker again later that turn.

Pinnacle Starcage's conversion costs {6}{W}{W}. It produces Robots only for cards actually moved from its exile to graveyards; exiled tokens cease to exist and produce none. Convert before artifact removal returns the imprisoned cards, when eight mana is available.

## 7. What beats it and how to adapt

Fast, distributed aggression punishes tapped lands and single-target exchanges. Favor early Seam Rip, then a sweeper; save broader removal for larger threats. Erode gives the opponent a tapped basic if available, accelerating future plays and weakening No More Lies. Get Lost gives two Maps, so its drawback matters more when creatures remain to explore.

Resilient threats, uncounterable spells, and repeated noncreature value punish reliance on counters or Day of Judgment. Preserve Erode for creatures or planeswalkers and Get Lost for those plus enchantments. Fatehold Charm can return a spell regardless of whether it can be countered, but buys time rather than permanently answering it.

Opposing Get Lost can undo Seam Rip, and artifact removal can undo Pinnacle Starcage. Counter release effects when the returning board is dangerous. Petrified Hamlet can suppress opposing Restless Anchorage, Fountainport, or Theorist's Sanctum activations, but naming affects your matching sources too; it does not remove their mana abilities.

## 8. Common mistakes

- Empowering The Theorist, Jace Beleren instead of a Jace token.
- Treating Jace's Machinations as permission for unlimited loyalty activations.
- Spending a token's last loyalty without accounting for its death.
- Exiling your own Illusions, Maps, or Fish with Pinnacle Starcage unnecessarily.
- Expecting Seam Rip or Pinnacle Starcage to exile anything if removed before its entry trigger resolves.
- Animating Restless Anchorage before Day of Judgment, exposing a land to your own sweeper.
- Using No More Lies after the opponent can comfortably pay {3}, instead of spending it earlier.
- Tapping out for selection or utility lands when protecting the established engine is worth more than another card.
