# Rakdos Lizards

Written 2026-10-11 by gpt-6.1-sol:high from the list at https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/7979160, cards generated 2026-10-10. Sideboardless alpha: main deck only. Advice for the pregame; the board and card text win any disagreement.

## 1. Archetype and game plan

Rakdos Lizards is synergy aggro: establish cheap attackers, turn small damage events into mana, counters, and temporary power, then finish through blockers with triggered damage and burn. Its 60 cards comprise 23 lands, 29 creatures, and eight instants. The creature curve is eight one-drops, 15 two-drops, and six three-drops; offspring adds a three-mana option.

The deck aims to threaten lethal around turns four through six, depending on interaction. Combat matters, but an opponent can stabilize the ground and still lose to Iridescent Vinelasher, Hired Claw, Gev, Scaled Scorch, and Ingris Stingerquill. Fireglass Mentor and Flamecache Gecko support continued pressure rather than a long-game control plan.

## 2. Engine and key interactions

**Enable life loss before spending on payoffs.** Hired Claw triggers when any Lizard attacks, even if Hired Claw stays home. Iridescent Vinelasher turns a land drop into an enabler without combat. Burst Lightning costs {R} to enable the engine, but spending a card this way should unlock meaningful development or lethal damage.

**Gev, Scaled Scorch into Flamecache Gecko:** with Gev, Scaled Scorch already present, cast Flamecache Gecko for {1}{R}. The cast trigger deals 1 before the creature resolves. Assuming that damage happens and Gev, Scaled Scorch remains, Flamecache Gecko enters as a 3/3, then its entry trigger supplies {B}{R}. Spend that mana on Fireglass Mentor or Master of Barbs, or another Flamecache Gecko to repeat the refund. The returned mana expires at the phase boundary (rule 106.4). This is net-zero mana expenditure, not free initial access to two mana.

**Counters and offspring:** in a normal two-player game, Gev, Scaled Scorch adds one counter to each other entering creature if the opponent lost life, not one counter per damage event. Iridescent Vinelasher with offspring costs {2}{B}. Its cast trigger from Gev, Scaled Scorch can enable both the original and the subsequent token: they enter as 2/3 and 2/2 respectively. The token receives its own counter; it does not copy the original's counter. Play the land afterward for two landfall triggers.

**Damage multiplication:** Valley Flamecaller increases damage from your Lizards, including their combat damage and triggered damage. It does not increase Burst Lightning or Stingerquill Charm damage. Ingris Stingerquill makes each attacking creature the damage source, so an attacking Lizard deals 2 through that trigger with Valley Flamecaller present; Ingris Stingerquill and Cadets still deal 1.

Master of Barbs rewards separate noncombat damage events. With it, Valley Flamecaller, Ingris Stingerquill, and Hired Claw present, attacking with two Lizards produces two attack-damage triggers plus Hired Claw's trigger: 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 damage and three team-wide +1/+0 effects, assuming everything resolves. These resolve before blockers. Master of Barbs must already be present to see an event; a Gev, Scaled Scorch cast trigger cannot pump the Master of Barbs still on the stack.

## 3. The curve and sequencing by turn

**Land order:** lead Mountain for Hired Claw or Swamp for Iridescent Vinelasher when possible. Blood Crypt provides either color immediately for 2 life and enables Blazemire Verge's red ability because it is both a Swamp and a Mountain. Blazemire Verge alone supplies black, not unrestricted red. Deploy Starting Town within your first three turns when practical; later it enters tapped.

**On the play, an illustrative sequence:**
- **Turn one:** Hired Claw for {R}.
- **Turn two:** attack, then cast Gev, Scaled Scorch for {B}{R}. This preserves the attack before committing the payoff.
- **Turn three:** cast Master of Barbs for {1}{R} before combat. Gev, Scaled Scorch enables its entry counter; subsequent attack triggers pump the team. Keep the remaining mana for Burst Lightning or a second-main one-drop.
- **Turn four:** cast Fireglass Mentor for {B}{R} before combat. After attack damage, its second-main trigger provides a choice; the remaining two mana can fund Flamecache Gecko and a refunded follow-up.
- **Turn five:** calculate triggered damage plus burn first. Otherwise add Valley Flamecaller for {2}{R}, use offspring, or extend through Fireglass Mentor without exposing every threat.

**On the draw:** turn one remains a one-drop, but turn two may require Burst Lightning before deploying another one-drop. Turn three can combine removal with a two-drop instead of tapping out for a three-drop. Turn four prioritizes a safe attack plus Fireglass Mentor or Flamecache Gecko development; turn five either converts reach into lethal or keeps Stingerquill Charm available for defense. Do not follow the curve mechanically into an opposing race you are losing.

## 4. Mulligans

A strong seven has two or three usable lands, a one-drop, and a two-drop payoff, with enough actual colored mana to cast them. Two lands plus Hired Claw, Gev, Scaled Scorch, and follow-up creatures is attractive; three lands plus Iridescent Vinelasher and Fireglass Mentor is also functional.

Ship zero-land hands, most one-land hands, five-land hands without unusually strong action, and hands whose apparent colors are unusable. Cavern of Souls naming Lizard cannot pay colored costs for Ingris Stingerquill or either instant. Rockface Village's red ability can cast Ingris Stingerquill, but cannot pay for burn. Four of the 23 lands, two Cavern of Souls and two Rockface Village, therefore require careful spell-specific accounting.

At six cards, retain a functioning curve rather than every engine piece. Usually bottom redundant expensive cards or surplus lands before the first enabler.

## 5. Combat and defense

Attack for triggered damage even when combat damage is uncertain, but compare the resulting damage with the cost of losing your engine. Hired Claw can remain back while another Lizard triggers it. Master of Barbs's menace pressures small boards, and its attack-trigger pumps can change blocking math before blockers are declared.

Stingerquill Charm for {B}{R} can give an attacker or blocker first strike and deathtouch. This threatens larger creatures without sacrificing your own, but removal in response can undo the exchange. Its 3-damage mode may simply clear the blocker more reliably.

Trade expendable creatures when racing requires it; protect Fireglass Mentor and damage multipliers when they represent several future cards or damage events. Ingris Stingerquill's 1/4 flying body can defend while other creatures attack. A Cadet is useful pressure or a disposable blocker, but is not a Lizard.

## 6. Resources and timing

Fireglass Mentor checks life loss as the second main phase begins (rule 603.4). Casting it after combat is too late that turn, and burning the opponent after the phase begins cannot create its missed trigger. Its chosen card expires at end of turn and still requires normal timing and costs. Preserve a land drop when that gives you a realistic chance to play an exiled land.

Hold {R} for Burst Lightning or {B}{R} for Stingerquill Charm when interaction outweighs another body. Kicked Burst Lightning costs five mana total for 4 damage. Flamecache Gecko's {1}{R}, discard-a-card activation exchanges cards rather than generating advantage.

Hired Claw's {1}{R} counter activation works once each turn after opposing life loss, including an opponent's turn. Rockface Village's haste activation needs {R} plus tapping the Village, only at sorcery speed. Ingris Stingerquill's {4} activation creates a Cadet and grants your creatures haste; before combat, it can convert fresh creatures into immediate attack triggers.

## 7. What beats it and how to adapt

Early removal disrupts the enabler-payoff chain; sweepers punish maximum deployment; large blockers constrain combat; lifegain extends the clock; faster aggression exploits painful lands and weak defensive starts.

Against removal, prefer sequences with immediate cast, landfall, or attack value. Against sweepers, retain a rebuilding creature and avoid unnecessary copies of legendary threats. Against blockers, emphasize triggered reach, flying, menace, and Stingerquill Charm. Against aggression, spend burn on creatures and avoid unnecessary Blood Crypt or Starting Town life payments. Cavern of Souls usually names Lizard against counters, but does not protect triggered abilities or noncreature spells.

## 8. Common mistakes

- Counting your own land payments as opposing life loss.
- Spending Flamecache Gecko's refund in a later phase.
- Expecting multiple counters from repeated damage under Gev, Scaled Scorch.
- Expecting Valley Flamecaller to amplify every damage source.
- Casting Fireglass Mentor after its trigger window.
- Spending restricted land mana on an ineligible spell or activation.
- Treating Gev, Scaled Scorch's ward as immunity: paying 2 life permits the targeting spell to proceed.
- Attacking automatically instead of calculating trigger damage, survival, and the opponent's return attack.
