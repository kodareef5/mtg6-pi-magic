# Mono-Black Aggro

Written 2026-10-11 by gpt-6.1-sol:high from the list at https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/7980837, cards generated 2026-10-10. Sideboardless alpha: main deck only. Advice for the pregame; the board and card text win any disagreement.

## 1. Archetype and game plan

Mono-Black Aggro combines early pressure, menace, and recurring Skeletons. It wants meaningful attacks from turn two and a decisive advantage around turns four or five, rather than a long defensive game. Dissection Practice and Iridescent Vinelasher finish opponents who stabilize combat at low life.

The strongest starts pair Nighthowl Pursuer with a four-power creature, or Forsaken Miner with Corpses of the Lost. The former makes blocking difficult; the latter keeps rebuilding attackers. There are 23 lands: 18 Swamp, three Soulstone Sanctuary, and two Realm of Koh. Twenty produce black mana, while five provide late-game threats. Spend early mana establishing pressure before leaning on those expensive land abilities.

## 2. Engine and key interactions

- **Skeleton pressure:** Corpses of the Lost costs {2}{B}, immediately supplies a 3/2 hasty Skeleton Pirate after its bonus, and makes Forsaken Miner a 3/2 with haste. If a permanent card entered your graveyard that turn, its end-step trigger lets you pay 1 life to return the enchantment. Recasting it costs another {2}{B} and leaves previous tokens intact. Returning it removes its bonus until you replay it; decline the return when sustained board strength matters more.
- **Crime recursion:** Dissection Practice costs {B} and always targets an opponent, so it enables Forsaken Miner even without creature targets. Pay an additional {B} per Miner trigger you want to use. One spell plus two returns therefore costs three black mana. Requiting Hex targeting an opposing creature, Sunset Saboteur targeting an opposing creature with its attack trigger, and Iridescent Vinelasher targeting an opponent with landfall also enable returns. A Miner already in the graveyard can return before the triggering spell resolves.
- **Landfall setup:** Cast Iridescent Vinelasher for {B}, or {2}{B} with offspring. Resolve the offspring trigger before playing a land to get two damage triggers, each a separate crime. Hold another {B} for each Miner return. A fourth land after spending three mana on offspring does not by itself fund that return unless it produces black.
- **Ferocious:** Sunset Saboteur supplies four power for Nighthowl Pursuer without needing to attack. Alternatively, pay 2 life to make Desolation Prowler 4/4 **before declaring attackers**. Lich's Relic costs {B} to cast and {2} to equip; an equipped Forsaken Miner is 4/3 even without Corpses of the Lost.
- **Removal package:** Lich's Relic costs three mana total to cast and pay for its entrance ability. Its resulting targeted destruction has no mana-value restriction. Equipping afterward costs another two, making the complete removal-and-equip sequence five mana. Requiting Hex costs {B}; optional blight adds a permanent -1/-1 counter to your creature, not mana, and gains 2 life when the spell resolves.

## 3. The curve and sequencing by turn

**On the play:** Turn one, lead Swamp into Forsaken Miner or Nighthowl Pursuer. Turn two, play Sunset Saboteur; a Pursuer can attack as a 3/3 because Saboteur already has four power. Desolation Prowler is an alternative, but reaching four power requires its life payment. Turn three, Corpses of the Lost adds an immediate attacker, or use Lich's Relic's three-mana removal package to clear a blocker. Turn four, combine another threat with removal or equip, leaving black mana for recursion when possible. Turn five, consider Soulstone Sanctuary if it improves the attack more than another spell.

**On the draw:** Turn one remains a creature unless Dissection Practice can remove a crucial one-toughness threat. Turn two, Requiting Hex plus another one-drop often beats tapping out for a two-drop into an established blocker. Turn three, choose immediate Skeleton pressure or removal according to the board. Turns four and five prioritize removing the blocker that enables a profitable double-block, then rebuilding pressure rather than racing blindly.

Lead Swamp before Realm of Koh so the latter enters untapped. Avoid leading Soulstone Sanctuary when your hand needs black on turn one. With an established Iridescent Vinelasher, play the land early for damage unless a creature death or a newly cast Vinelasher makes waiting more valuable.

## 4. Mulligans

Keep seven-card hands with two or three lands, an early black source, a one-drop, and a coherent follow-up: four-power support, Corpses of the Lost, or useful interaction. Twelve creatures cost one mana; ten cost two, so an opening without early pressure is not representative of the deck's strengths.

Ship landless hands, hands whose only land is Soulstone Sanctuary, and slow hands full of mana sinks but no attackers. A one-Swamp hand with several one-drops is a calculated risk, more tolerable on the draw; it still needs a second land for most productive development. Four lands can be acceptable with strong early creatures and a utility land, but not with only reactive spells. When going to six, preserve functional mana and pressure before redundant expensive plays.

## 5. Combat and defense

Attack aggressively when menace forces unfavorable double-blocks or removal can break the opponent's blocking structure. Sunset Saboteur's attack trigger strengthens an opposing creature before blockers: choose the least relevant target, not automatically the smallest. With no opposing creature available to target, that trigger supplies no crime.

Forsaken Miner cannot block. It is a preferred attacker or trade when recursion is available, but do not count it toward defensive coverage. Preserve a four-power enabler when losing it would weaken future Pursuer attacks. Desolation Prowler can pump once on your turn and again on the opponent's turn, making it a useful defensive anchor when life permits.

Trading an actual creature card can enable descent; losing a Skeleton Pirate token cannot. Hold back when an opposing crack-back is lethal or when another menace creature next turn changes otherwise poor attacks into profitable ones.

## 6. Resources and timing

Untapped {B} represents Requiting Hex, Dissection Practice, or one Miner return, not all three. Budget recursion before spending your final black source. Iridescent Vinelasher's landfall trigger lets you use the newly played land for a return if that land enters untapped and produces black. Sunset Saboteur's attack trigger similarly rewards retaining black mana through combat.

Soulstone Sanctuary's activation costs {4}, does not tap it, and has no stated duration. Usually five lands let four pay while Sanctuary attacks. It needs to have been under your control since your turn began unless haste applies. Its all-creature-types ability makes it a Skeleton: one Corpses of the Lost turns it into a 4/3 with haste. Realm of Koh costs {3}{B} plus tapping itself, usually committing five lands; its Spirit is an evasive finisher, not a blocker against ordinary creatures.

Descent must already have happened when your end step begins. It remains satisfied even if the permanent card subsequently leaves your graveyard. Gollum, Riddle Master rewards predicting opposing spell parity; choose odd or even from known costs. Each of its three modes is available only once during that battlefield stay. Take cards for continued pressure or drain when racing.

## 7. What beats it and how to adapt

Wide creature boards defeat menace, while large blockers exceed Requiting Hex's restriction. Save Lich's Relic for the latter and use cheaper interaction to reduce double-block options. Against aggressive decks, protect life from unnecessary Desolation Prowler payments and Corpses of the Lost returns; Dissection Practice and optional blight can help stabilize.

Sweepers punish deploying every creature. Hold a follow-up threat, preserve utility lands, and leave a route to a crime after Forsaken Miner dies. Exile removal and graveyard hate undermine recursion; pivot toward fresh bodies, offspring, and land threats rather than spending turns waiting for an unavailable engine. Enchantment removal reduces Skeleton damage, so reassess combat totals immediately. These are sequencing adjustments; no sideboard was supplied.

## 8. Common mistakes

Do not pump Desolation Prowler after attacks and expect a missed Pursuer trigger. Do not count token deaths, instant cards, or opposing deaths as your descent. Blighting Forsaken Miner once merely makes it 1/1; it does not kill it. Do not forget Sunset Saboteur's compulsory opposing counter, its ward discard cost when opponents target it, or that ward does not protect against untargeted sweepers. Finally, do not automatically return Corpses of the Lost: losing its bonus during the opponent's turn can cost more than another token is worth.
