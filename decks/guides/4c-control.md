# 4c Control

Written 2026-10-11 by gpt-6.1-sol:high from the list at https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/7954765, cards generated 2026-10-10. Sideboardless alpha: main deck only. Advice for the pregame; the board and card text win any disagreement.

## 1. Archetype and game plan

4c Control is a tap-out/instant-speed hybrid: trade early, establish extra mana and repeatable cards, then dominate with sweepers and expensive spells. The supplied list has **61 cards: 24 lands and 37 nonlands**, including six mana artifacts. It wants a long game, not an early damage race.

Its central advantage is Unholy Annex // Ritual Chamber backed by Firdoch Core: draw every end step while draining rather than losing life. Finish through repeated drains, a flying Demon, Thor, God of Thunder, animated permanents, or burn from the expensive spells. Stabilization usually begins around turns four and five; Jeskai Revelation becomes the major late-game swing. Against it, pressure the setup turns rather than letting its extra cards and mana accumulate.

## 2. Engine and key interactions

- **Firdoch Core plus Unholy Annex // Ritual Chamber:** pay {3} for the artifact, then {2}{B} for Unholy Annex. Changeling makes the unanimated artifact a Demon, so no {4} animation is needed for the drain. It is also a Dragon on the battlefield or in hand: choose it or reveal it when casting Dispelling Exhale for {1}{U}, increasing the tax from {2} to {4}. An unanimated Core survives Deadly Cover-Up.
- **Room sequencing:** opening Ritual Chamber costs {3}{B}{B}, either by casting that half initially or unlocking it later at sorcery speed. Annex first supplies cards; Chamber first supplies a blocker and clock without immediate card draw. Unlocking is not casting a spell and does not trigger Thor, God of Thunder. Plan around losing the Demon or Core before an Annex trigger resolves.
- **Acceleration:** Firdoch Core can tap immediately while it is not a creature. Tablet of Discovery costs {2}{R} and can immediately tap for {R}{R} toward instants and sorceries, but only {R} toward other expenditures. Five lands plus Tablet provide seven mana for Jeskai Revelation; five lands plus Core provide six for Together as One. Check colored requirements separately.
- **Converge:** Together as One costs {6}. Four colors spent produce four cards, four damage, and four life; five produce five of each. Firdoch Core or Great Hall of the Biblioplex can supply green despite the four-color land base. Colorless mana adds no color, and Tablet's {R}{R} counts as only one color.
- **Graveyard choices:** Deadly Cover-Up costs {3}{B}{B}; evidence requires exiling at least six mana value from your own graveyard. Stock Up plus Shoot the Sheriff plus Requiting Hex supplies 3 + 2 + 1 = 6. After destruction, a newly killed opposing creature can supply the name to extract. Preserve valuable recursion targets when possible.
- **Recursion and reach:** Flashback costs {R}, followed by the target's normal mana cost: replaying Jeskai Revelation costs eight total. Thor, God of Thunder costs {3}{R}{R}, then permits its exiled card to be played through your next turn, still paying costs and respecting timing. A subsequent Jeskai Revelation triggers seven Thor damage in addition to its own effects.

## 3. The curve and sequencing by turn

**Land priorities:** lead an otherwise inconvenient tapped land when turn one needs no answer. Blood Crypt enables Blazemire Verge, Riverpyre Verge, and Mistrise Village; Watery Grave enables Gloomlake Verge and Riverpyre Verge. Other typed shock lands similarly unlock the relevant Verge conditions. Sundown Pass is best from turn three onward. Avoid paying shock-land life without a concrete reason.

Great Hall of the Biblioplex fixes spell colors, not every expenditure: its colored mana cannot cast Firdoch Core, Tablet of Discovery, Unholy Annex // Ritual Chamber, or Thor, God of Thunder, nor pay an unlock or animation cost. It can supply generic costs through its colorless ability.

**Typical play-side sequence:**

1. Develop a typed land, tapped if safe; retain access to {B} if Requiting Hex is needed.
2. Hold {1}{B} or {1}{U}. Use Consult the Star Charts at the opponent's end step only if removal or Dispelling Exhale is unnecessary.
3. Cast Firdoch Core if the board permits; its immediate mana can leave Requiting Hex available. Otherwise answer threats or use Stock Up to find land.
4. Four lands plus Core provide five mana: cast Unholy Annex for three and retain two for interaction.
5. Five lands plus Core provide six: sweep for five, open Ritual Chamber for five, or cast Together as One for six according to the board.

**On the draw:** prioritize turn-one Requiting Hex and turn-two Shoot the Sheriff over selection. Turn three is a stabilization decision, not an automatic artifact turn. On turn four, keeping {1}{R}{W}{B} for Inevitable Defeat may be better than developing Annex. Turn five often belongs to Deadly Cover-Up; deploy the engine only when incoming damage allows it.

## 4. Mulligans

Keep sevens with roughly three lands, functioning early black or blue, an early answer, and selection or an engine piece. Two lands can work with live interaction and Consult the Star Charts, but Consult only selects one card unless kicked; it does not guarantee another land.

Ship one-land hands, hands dominated by seven-mana spells, and nominally land-rich hands whose colors cannot cast their spells. Multiple Great Hall of the Biblioplex can support removal yet strand Annex. Against pressure, a keep without cheap interaction needs an unusually convincing recovery plan. Against slower decks, prioritize sustained cards and dependable mana over redundant creature removal.

## 5. Combat and defense

Early life is a resource, but shocks, Great Hall of the Biblioplex, and an unsupported Annex can exhaust it quickly. Take small hits when they preserve a stronger sweep; remove threats whose damage or abilities make waiting dangerous.

Use Ritual Chamber's Demon defensively until racing is clearly favorable. Jeskai Revelation's Monks can chump, trade, or grow through subsequent noncreature spells. They do not get prowess triggers from the Revelation that created them.

Firdoch Core's {4} animation is an emergency block or late attack, not routine development. It becomes vulnerable to creature removal and sweepers. A newly entered Core cannot attack or use its tap ability after becoming a creature. Avoid trading your only Demon if that turns Annex into recurring life loss.

## 6. Resources and timing

Reserve {B} for Requiting Hex, {1}{B} for Shoot the Sheriff, {1}{U} for Dispelling Exhale, or all four colors for Inevitable Defeat. Hex handles only mana value two or less; Sheriff cannot kill outlaws. Animated Firdoch Core is itself an outlaw through changeling.

Kick Consult the Star Charts for {2}{U}{U} when four mana can remain unused safely; its selection depth counts lands, not artifacts. Tablet of Discovery's milled card is playable only that turn, with normal costs and timing. Save the land drop when an immediately usable milled land could matter.

Mistrise Village requires {U} and tapping the land to protect the next spell that turn, effectively consuming two mana sources before casting it. Jeskai Revelation can return a spell even if that spell cannot be countered. Hold it through the opponent's decisive commitment when possible, rather than spending seven mana on minor tempo.

## 7. What beats it and how to adapt

Fast aggression punishes artifact setup and unsupported Annex. Counter with cheap removal, conservative life payments, and timely sweepers. Wide boards favor Deadly Cover-Up; isolated nonland engines favor Inevitable Defeat.

Artifact removal breaks both acceleration and the Demon/Dragon support. Enchantment removal stops repeated cards. Preserve alternative routes: Ritual Chamber, Thor, God of Thunder, and direct spell damage. Graveyard hate weakens Flashback, Thor's recovery, and evidence, so do not build a plan requiring all three.

Countermagic punishes expensive commitments; use Mistrise Village where its extra mana is affordable. Opposing recursion rewards exile and evidence extraction. No sideboard is supplied, so these adjustments concern sequencing and resource allocation, not invented substitutions.

## 8. Common mistakes

- Animating Firdoch Core before Deadly Cover-Up, destroying your own engine.
- Treating Great Hall of the Biblioplex's colored mana as unrestricted.
- Counting artifact mana as lands for Consult the Star Charts.
- Assuming Tablet's restricted {R}{R} can pay Room unlocks.
- Counting two red mana as two converge colors.
- Spending recursion targets carelessly on evidence.
- Treating Flashback as free or as permission to cast sorceries at instant speed.
- Activating Great Hall of the Biblioplex before a sweep: its {5} animation has no end-of-turn expiration and leaves the land vulnerable to creature destruction.
