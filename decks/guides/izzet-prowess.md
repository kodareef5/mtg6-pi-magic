## 1. Archetype and game plan

Izzet Prowess is spell-driven tempo aggro: establish threats, turn cheap selection and interaction into combat damage, then finish through flying, burn, or Gandalf, Goblins' Bane triggers. Strong draws threaten wins around turns four through six; slower games lean on Drake tokens, card selection, and Eddymurk Crab rather than one oversized attack.

The 60 cards comprise 20 lands, 18 creatures, and 22 noncreature spells: 11 instants, nine sorceries, and two Equipment. Deploy creatures before spending spells when practical. Multiple threats multiply each spell's damage; selection also fills the graveyard to improve Flow State and discount Eddymurk Crab.

## 4. Mulligans

Keep most functional two- or three-land hands with an early creature and cheap selection or interaction. Check actual colors, not merely land count. Elusive Otter wants immediate blue; Slickshot Show-Off and removal need red. Against aggression, interaction and Drake Hatcher improve a keep; against slower decks, threat density and Spell Pierce matter more.

Ship landless hands, hands dominated by expensive payoffs without setup, and hands unable to cast their early plays. A one-land hand with selection is risky, especially on the play; a red-only Verge cannot cast Opt. Four lands usually require a convincing threat-and-selection package. When bottoming, trim surplus lands or redundant expensive cards before sacrificing the hand's functioning engine.

## 5. Combat and defense

Attack when spell bonuses, evasion, and removal make the damage worthwhile. Increase Elusive Otter's power before blockers are declared to exclude smaller blockers; pumping afterward does not undo an existing block. Likewise, use Crab's tap trigger before blocker declaration. None of these attackers has printed trample: killing a blocker after blocks does not make the blocked attacker hit the player.

Drake Hatcher is the best routine attacker-defender because of vigilance. Preserve it when repeated connections promise tokens. Drake tokens can trade or absorb attacks when that protects the engine, but retain flyers when they are the reliable finishing route. Heroes' Hangout's Patrol Night provides first strike and extra power to up to two creatures; alongside prowess, it can turn apparent trades into clean wins.

Eddymurk Crab cast during the opponent's turn enters tapped and cannot immediately block. Cast it before attackers are declared to tap potential attackers, or at end step to untap with a 5/5. Tapping an already attacking creature does not remove it from combat.

## 7. What beats it and how to adapt

Cheap removal before spell chains, sweepers, graveyard disruption, and large blockers attack different parts of the engine. Against removal, spread investment across threats and preserve Spell Pierce rather than routinely tapping out for Equipment. Against sweepers, retain a creature or plotted Slickshot Show-Off as a rebuild; do not empty the hand for marginal damage.

Graveyard disruption weakens Flow State and makes Crab expensive. Reassess both before committing mana. Against large ground blockers, emphasize flyers, Otter's pre-block power increases, and Crab's tapping. Against fast aggression, prioritize removal, vigilance, and life-efficient lands over maximum goldfish damage. These are main-deck adjustments; no sideboard was supplied.

## 8. Common mistakes

Avoid casting setup spells before deploying payoffs without a reason; counting Adventures as graveyard sorceries; casting a plotted creature immediately; treating equip as a spell; or assuming Crab is an opposing-turn blocker. Do not sacrifice essential fixing for speculative Grove's Bounty value. Finally, count actual triggers and mana, not hoped-for damage: the explosive Staff lines only begin after attachment, and a blocked attacker still needs a way through.
