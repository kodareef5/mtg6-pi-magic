# Selesnya Landfall

Written 2026-10-11 by gpt-6.1-sol:high from the list at https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/7954762, cards generated 2026-10-10. Sideboardless alpha: main deck only. Advice for the pregame; the board and card text win any disagreement.

## 1. Archetype and game plan

Selesnya Landfall is a ramp-combat deck with explosive, potentially lethal turns around turns four and five. Establish a growing attacker, accelerate mana, then convert multiple land entries into counters, power doubling, and trample. Sazh's Chocobo supplies early pressure; Mossborn Hydra supplies exponential growth; Mightform Harmonizer turns an established creature into a finisher. Earthbender Ascension supplies both acceleration and eventual evasion.

The slower plan uses Icetill Explorer to recycle fetch lands and Dyadrine, Synthesis Amalgam to turn counters into cards and bodies. This is not an all-in combo: preserve enough material to threaten another attack after removal.

## 2. Engine and key interactions

- **Fetch engine:** Fabled Passage and Escape Tunnel each produce two land entries when played and sacrificed for a basic. With Icetill Explorer, play a fetch, sacrifice it, replay it from the graveyard as your additional land, and sacrifice again: four entries. Casting Icetill Explorer costs {2}{G}{G}; these land plays and fetch activations cost no mana. Its milling can supply later land drops or Esper Origins, but does not guarantee either.
- **Growth into doubling:** Cast Mossborn Hydra for {2}{G}, then make land drops; its counters progress 1 → 2 → 4 → 8. Cast Mightform Harmonizer for {2}{G}{G}, or warp it for {2}{G}, before your payoff land entries. Resolve counter growth before the corresponding power-doubling trigger. Doubling adds power based on power at resolution; later growth is not retroactively doubled. Multiple doubling triggers can target the same attacker.
- **Quest payoff:** Earthbender Ascension costs {2}{G}, earthbends an existing land, and fetches a tapped basic. That basic supplies its first quest counter. The fourth and every subsequent quest-counter addition produces a separate targeted counter-and-trample trigger. Order those growth triggers ahead of doubling when practical.
- **Earthbend recycling:** An earthbent fetch land can be sacrificed to fetch, then return tapped through earthbend. The searched basic and returned land both trigger landfall. The returned land is a new, unanimated permanent without its old counters. Ba Sing Se's activation costs {2}{G} plus tapping Ba Sing Se: effectively four mana sources committed, not three.
- **Evasion:** Activate Escape Tunnel targeting a creature with power 2 or less, and let that ability resolve before enlarging it. Later growth does not remove the blocking restriction. This activation sacrifices the land but does not fetch a basic.
- **Secondary value:** Dyadrine, Synthesis Amalgam with X=0 costs {G}{W}, enters with two counters, and is 2/3. Its attack trigger removes one counter from each of two different creatures, which need not be attackers. Esper Origins costs {1}{G}; flashing it back costs {3}{G} and produces Summon: Esper Maduin. Chapter I replaces material, chapter II supplies main-phase mana, and chapter III prepares a team-wide trample attack before the Saga is sacrificed and exiled by finality.

## 3. The curve and sequencing by turn

**On the play, an accelerated example:**

1. Forest into Llanowar Elves.
2. A second Forest; spend both lands and Llanowar Elves on Earthbender Ascension. Earthbend a used land and fetch another Forest.
3. Your three existing lands plus Llanowar Elves cast Icetill Explorer before a land drop. Play Fabled Passage, fetch, replay it, and fetch again. You finish with five lands; both searched basics untap because each search leaves at least four lands. Four entries advance Earthbender Ascension from one quest counter to five.
4. Cast Mightform Harmonizer before land drops, then multiply an established attacker. Normal casting retains the body; warp saves one mana for another commitment.
5. Assemble another burst or use Erode to clear the decisive blocker. Do not force lethal through an obvious answer if recycling lands can win next turn.

**On the draw, a steadier example:** Turn one Sazh's Chocobo; turn two play your second land and cast Shared Roots for {1}{G}, ending with three lands. Turn three cast Mossborn Hydra or Earthbender Ascension before your land drop. Turn four cast Icetill Explorer before using its two land plays. Turn five deploy Mightform Harmonizer and attack. Against immediate pressure, replace ramp with Erode and accept a slower clock.

Lead Forest where possible: it enables Llanowar Elves and makes Ba Sing Se untapped. Temple Garden enables either color but is not a basic land. Pay its 2 life only when untapped mana changes your turn. Preserve fetches when their future triggers outweigh immediate fixing.

## 4. Mulligans

The deck has 26 lands, including 11 basics, and seven fetch lands: four Fabled Passage plus three Escape Tunnel. A keepable seven usually contains two or three lands, early green, and either Llanowar Elves or a coherent growth/ramp sequence. Four lands can work with Icetill Explorer and a payoff.

Ship hands dominated by four-mana cards without acceleration, hands unable to produce green promptly, and hands whose only development is tapped fetching. One-land Llanowar Elves hands are fragile, especially on the play: removal or a missed draw strands the rest. White-only starts do not support this deck's core.

## 5. Combat and defense

Attack with Sazh's Chocobo when trades are favorable, but preserve it when upcoming land entries make it your best finisher. Mossborn Hydra already has trample; a huge Chocobo needs Earthbender Ascension, Escape Tunnel, or chapter III of Summon: Esper Maduin to avoid a chump block.

Earthbent lands can attack immediately because they have haste, provided they are untapped. Losing one to death or exile generally returns the land tapped, making it a useful defensive body; bounce does not trigger that return. Blocking still sacrifices tempo and removes accumulated counters.

Llanowar Elves usually belongs behind the attack until mana stops mattering. Robot tokens are expendable blockers. Avoid trading Icetill Explorer casually: its ongoing land access is often worth more than two combat damage.

## 6. Resources and timing

Keep {W} available for Erode when a blocker or opposing threat can change the race. There are seven direct white-producing lands, counting Hushwood Verge's conditional white ability; fetches also find Plains. Secure white without unnecessarily sacrificing early green development.

Erode exchanges an opposing threat for possible tapped-land acceleration and landfall. Self-targeting an earthbent creature-land can generate land entries, but is a situational resource conversion, not the default use of removal.

Fabled Passage checks your land count after the fetched basic enters. Escape Tunnel's fetched basic never receives that untap clause. Icetill Explorer grants two total land plays per turn, not two extra plays; removing it can prevent a still-unused second play.

Warped Mightform Harmonizer has no haste. It normally boosts an older attacker, leaves at the next end step, and can be cast from exile on a later turn for its normal cost. Spend Summon: Esper Maduin's chapter-II mana in the precombat main phase; it does not wait until combat.

## 7. What beats it and how to adapt

Early removal on Llanowar Elves disrupts accelerated starts. Instant-speed removal on the intended doubled attacker wastes concentrated investment. Against open interaction, distribute counters, retain another threat, and delay sacrificing fetches until the relevant creature has resolved.

Sweepers punish excessive creature deployment. Hold a follow-up threat and exploit surviving Earthbender Ascension or warped cards. Earthbent lands recover from death or exile, but their counters and creature status do not. Graveyard hate shuts down recycled fetches and Esper Origins flashback; respond by valuing natural land drops and threats already available.

Fast attackers punish setup turns and Temple Garden life payments. Prioritize Erode, sturdy blocks, and Esper Origins' life gain. These are sequencing adaptations; no sideboard was supplied.

## 8. Common mistakes

- Playing lands before deploying the creature that needs their triggers.
- Treating Earthbender Ascension as granting trample before its fourth quest counter.
- Expecting warp to grant haste or permit an immediate same-turn recast from exile.
- Growing an Escape Tunnel target above two power before its ability resolves.
- Spending the same land's tap on both mana and an activated ability.
- Removing Mossborn Hydra's last counter for Dyadrine, Synthesis Amalgam without accounting for the Hydra dying.
- Assuming milling a land grants an additional land play, or that a returned earthbent land retains counters.
