# Dimir Excruciator

Written 2026-10-11 by gpt-6.1-sol:high from the list at https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/7954759, cards generated 2026-10-10. Sideboardless alpha: main deck only. Advice for the pregame; the board and card text win any disagreement.

## 1. Archetype and game plan

Dimir Excruciator is a control-combo deck with a credible creature finish. Cheap removal and discard buy time, selection assembles the engine, and Doomsday Excruciator reduces both libraries to six cards. Restless Reef then mills the opponent toward a failed draw. Superior Spider-Man can deliver the same library reduction for four mana by entering as a copy of a discarded Doomsday Excruciator: Superior Spider-Man was cast, so the copied enters ability triggers.

The fast line resets libraries on turn four; the natural six-mana line usually starts on turn six or later. Neither is automatically lethal. Count the opponent's remaining draws, your own extra upkeep draws, and available attacks before committing. Deceit, Emeritus of Ideation, and flying copies also let the deck win through damage without resetting libraries.

## 2. Engine and key interactions

- **Graveyard setup:** Bitter Triumph costs {1}{B}; discarding Doomsday Excruciator pays its additional cost while removing a creature or planeswalker. Winternight Stories costs {2}{U}, draws three, and can discard just Doomsday Excruciator because it is a creature. Undercity Sewers can surveil it into the graveyard without spending spell mana.
- **Four-mana reset:** Cast Superior Spider-Man for {2}{U}{B}, copying Doomsday Excruciator in a graveyard. The result is a 4/4 with flying and the copied upkeep draw ability; the source card is exiled. This is a cast creature entering, not an uncast reanimation. Protect the graveyard card until Superior Spider-Man resolves.
- **Deceit bridge:** Evoke Deceit for {B}{B} to take a nonland card, or {U}{U} to bounce another nonland permanent. Paying {U}{B} satisfies neither condition. It is sacrificed through its evoke trigger afterward. Later, Superior Spider-Man can copy it without being sacrificed, since no evoke cost was paid. Paying Superior Spider-Man's four-mana cost with {U}{U}{B}{B} enables both copied enters abilities; {1}{U}{B}{B} enables discard only.
- **Library finish:** Restless Reef costs {2}{U}{B} to animate, and its attack trigger mills four regardless of whether combat damage connects. With five lands, the other four can animate an established Restless Reef. An empty library is not itself lethal; attempting to draw from it is.
- **Prepared finish:** Emeritus of Ideation enters prepared, allowing one copy of Ancestral Recall to be cast for {U} while it remains prepared on the battlefield. Target yourself for resources before the reset, or target an opponent with fewer than three library cards to force a failed draw. It is not a separate one-mana spell available directly from hand. An attack can prepare it again by exiling eight graveyard cards.

Winternight Stories harmonizes for {4}{U}; tapping a creature with power four or greater reduces this to {U}. This works with a newly entered creature, but consumes its availability for combat. Stock Up puts cards into hand rather than drawing them and lets you order three cards on the bottom, valuable preparation for Doomsday Excruciator's six-card library.

## 3. The curve and sequencing by turn

**On the play:** Turn one usually establishes Undercity Sewers or Restless Reef if no immediate interaction is needed; otherwise lead Swamp or an untapped Watery Grave for Duress or Requiting Hex. Turn two holds Bitter Triumph or evokes Deceit to disrupt and establish a copy target. Turn three uses Stock Up or Winternight Stories if the board permits. Turn four casts Superior Spider-Man for the best available engine or stabilizing copy. Turn five attacks with an established Restless Reef after a reset, or develops Emeritus of Ideation or Harvester of Misery. The natural Doomsday Excruciator line follows at six usable black mana.

**On the draw:** Prioritize surviving the opponent's second and third turns. Lead an untapped black source when Requiting Hex matters, leave Bitter Triumph available on turn two, and delay turn-three selection if it would surrender the board. Day of Black Sun for X=2 costs four mana and can replace the turn-four creature against a wide cheap board. Turn five may require Deadly Cover-Up rather than a threat.

Swamp or Watery Grave enables Gloomlake Verge's black ability; Gloomlake Verge itself has neither basic land type. Place tapped lands early when possible. Preserve an established Restless Reef for the attack after the reset rather than relying on a newly played land to attack.

## 4. Mulligans

The list has 26 lands: 10 Swamp, four each of Gloomlake Verge, Restless Reef, and Watery Grave, and two each of Cavern of Souls and Undercity Sewers. Keep most sevens with three lands, early interaction, and either selection or a useful creature line. Two-land hands need functional colors, cheap defense, and realistic access to the third land; turn-three selection cannot rescue a missed third land by itself.

Ship hands crowded with expensive creatures, all-tapped early mana against aggression, or Gloomlake Verge/Cavern of Souls combinations that cannot cast the black noncreature spells. A reset piece without defense or a finishing plan is not sufficient. Bottom redundant expensive threats before essential lands or interaction.

## 5. Combat and defense

Before resetting, trade life for time only when the next turn genuinely stabilizes. Hard-cast Deceit and Harvester of Misery can anchor the ground; flying threats pressure over it. Copying Harvester of Misery with Superior Spider-Man gives another sweep of other creatures for -2/-2, but also shrinks your own supporting creatures.

After resetting, Restless Reef's mill often matters more than its survival. Attack into a blocker when the trigger creates a winning draw clock, but do not expose it to removal before attackers are declared without considering the lost mana and land. Its deathtouch also makes it a strong defensive trade when survival takes priority.

Archenemy's Charm's counters and temporary lifelink can swing a damage race. Requiting Hex's optional blight gains two life, but permanently weakens your creature; avoid making a vital blocker too small just for that gain.

## 6. Resources and timing

Reserve {B} for Requiting Hex, {1}{B} for Bitter Triumph or Harvester of Misery's discard ability, and {B}{B}{B} for Archenemy's Charm. These answer threats, not opposing spells. Duress must clear protection before committing; it cannot take creatures, whereas Deceit's black trigger can take any nonland card.

Cavern of Souls naming Demon protects Doomsday Excruciator and supplies one of its six black mana. Naming Spider protects Superior Spider-Man even when it will copy another creature after resolving. Neither choice supplies colored mana for ordinary removal or selection spells.

Do not double-book mana: casting Superior Spider-Man and animating Restless Reef in one turn requires eight mana plus the attacking land. After resetting, each surviving Doomsday Excruciator ability adds an upkeep draw beyond your normal draw. Recount both libraries before using Winternight Stories or Ancestral Recall on yourself.

## 7. What beats it and how to adapt

Fast aggression punishes tapped lands, setup turns, and life payments. Keep cheap removal, use Day of Black Sun at the relevant mana-value threshold, and favor Harvester of Misery copies over a premature reset. Flying attackers and direct damage can make a reduced library irrelevant if you die first.

Graveyard exile stops Superior Spider-Man's intended copy. Pivot to hard-cast threats, discard the graveyard answer before setup when possible, or recover creatures with Archenemy's Charm before they are exiled. Countermagic punishes expensive commitments; sequence Duress and the appropriate Cavern of Souls choice around it.

Removal of Restless Reef or Emeritus of Ideation can strand the library plan. Against those answers, establish another finisher or keep winning through damage. Conversely, an opponent should pressure life totals while disrupting those outlets rather than assume removing Doomsday Excruciator restores exiled libraries.

## 8. Common mistakes

- Resetting without calculating your faster self-decking clock.
- Expecting one Restless Reef attack to guarantee an immediate loss: six cards minus one draw minus four milled leaves one.
- Treating Ancestral Recall as freely castable from hand, or forgetting preparation is consumed.
- Spending mixed evoke mana on Deceit and expecting either trigger.
- Using Cavern of Souls for colored noncreature costs.
- Forgetting that copying preserves the copied mana cost: Superior Spider-Man copying Doomsday Excruciator has mana value six, despite being a 4/4.
- Sweeping away your own finishing creatures without a replacement plan.
